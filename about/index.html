About

Hi, I'm Olga

I founded ReMeet because I believe your photos deserve to become a real book, not sit forgotten on your phone.

I'm a designer and a mom, based in Porto, Portugal. I started ReMeet after realizing I had thousands of photos on my phone and zero time to organize them into a book.

Every online editor felt like a part-time job: pick photos, crop them, drag them onto templates, adjust text boxes, repeat for forty pages.

I built ReMeet so you never touch an editor. You share a link to your photos, I curate the best shots and design every page, and you get a finished book in 72 hours.

How ReMeet works

  1. Pick your book type and page count on remeet.cc
  2. Share a link to your photos (iCloud, Google Photos, any cloud folder)
  3. Receive a designed proof in 72 hours
  4. Approve, download your digital book, or order a printed hardcover
