About

The person behind your book

ReMeet was started by one person who noticed that most family photo books never get finished.

Olga Miller

Founder of ReMeet. Based in Porto, Portugal.

I spent years working as a creative director, designing everything from brand identities to advertising campaigns. Along the way I noticed something: almost everyone I knew had thousands of photos on their phone and zero printed albums. Not because they didn't care, but because the process of making a photo book takes too long. You open the editor, spend an hour dragging photos into grids, give up on page 12, and close the tab. The book never gets finished.

ReMeet exists to fix that. You share a link to your photos. I take care of the rest: selecting the best shots, removing duplicates, designing every page, and sending you a finished proof within 72 hours. AI helps with the sorting, but a human designer checks every single page before you see it.

How ReMeet works

  1. You share a link. iCloud, Google Photos, or any cloud album. Send as many photos as you want (up to 5,000).
  2. AI sorts your photos. It scores every image for sharpness, composition, and duplicates. The best ones make it into the book.
  3. A designer builds your book. Real layouts, not templates. Full-bleed pages, varied spreads, handwritten-style captions.
  4. You get a proof in 72 hours. Review it, request changes in plain language, and approve when you're happy.
  5. Print or keep digital. Download the PDF or order a printed hardcover shipped to your door.

Every book is checked by a person

AI is good at sorting thousands of photos and spotting blurry frames. It is not good at knowing which moment matters most to your family. That's why every ReMeet book is reviewed by a human designer who checks crops, fixes text placement, and makes sure no face is cut off. The result is a book that looks like you spent a weekend making it, except you spent five minutes.

Why Porto

ReMeet is based in Porto, Portugal, and serves customers worldwide. Most clients are in the United States and Canada. Printed books ship from a US-based print partner, so domestic shipping is fast and affordable.
