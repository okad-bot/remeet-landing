Chapters that honor your journey

Every adoption story has its own shape. Here are some chapters families often include — choose the ones that feel true to yours:

  • The decision — How and why your family chose adoption. The conversations, the research, the moment it became real
  • The process — Home studies, paperwork milestones, agency visits. The behind-the-scenes work that made it happen
  • The wait — The hardest part for many families. Photos from this period capture hope, patience, and preparation
  • Preparing for you — Setting up the room, buying first clothes, the family getting ready for someone they hadn't met yet
  • Travel — For international adoptions: the flights, the new country, the hotels, navigating an unfamiliar place with purpose
  • Meeting day — The moment your family became whole. However it looked, however it felt — this is the heart of the book
  • First days together — Learning each other. First meals, first bath, first night at home. The beginning of everything
  • Becoming family — The days and weeks after, settling in, meeting extended family, the routines that started to form
  • Letters and reflections — Words you want your child to read someday. What you felt, what you hoped, what you want them to know

Designed with sensitivity

We know that adoption stories carry complexity. Some details are deeply personal. Some parts of the journey were painful. Some families want to include birth family information; others don't.

You decide what goes in. We never assume what your story should look like. During the design process, you'll review every page, every caption, every photo choice. If something doesn't feel right, we change it. No questions asked.

Language matters. We use the terminology you prefer. We follow your lead on how to frame each chapter. The book reflects your family's voice, not ours.

What to include

Photos

  • Paperwork milestones — the approval letter, the completed dossier, the finalization documents
  • Travel photos — airports, hotels, landscapes, meals in a new place
  • The first meeting — however many photos you have, even if they're blurry or imperfect
  • First family photos — the official and unofficial ones
  • Homecoming — the airport arrival, the front door, the room that was waiting
  • Daily life — the ordinary moments that became extraordinary because you were finally together

Beyond photos

  • Letters to your child — written before, during, or after the process
  • Maps showing the journey — where you traveled, where your child was born
  • Timeline of milestones — dates that matter to your family's story
  • Quotes or poems that held meaning during the process

Why this book matters

Children who were adopted often want to understand their story. They ask questions. They want to see the photos, read the letters, know the details. A thoughtfully made adoption photo book gives them something tangible — a story they can hold, revisit, and carry with them.

This isn't just a photo book. It's an origin story. Your child's origin story, told with honesty and love.
