How the AI photo book maker works

You share your photos. Our AI does everything a professional designer would — but in minutes, not days. Here's what happens behind the scenes when you use Remeet's AI photo book creator.

Photo analysis

Every image is scored for technical quality (sharpness, exposure, composition) and content (faces, expressions, landmarks, food, nature). Blurry duplicates are quietly set aside. The best version of each moment rises to the top.

Smart grouping

Photos are clustered by date, location, and the people in them. A birthday party becomes a chapter. A weekend trip gets its own spread. The AI recognizes events the way you remember them — as stories, not timestamps.

Editorial design

Layout, pacing, and typography are chosen based on what's in the photos. A panoramic landscape gets a full-bleed spread. A series of candid moments gets a grid. The book flows like a magazine, not a slideshow.

Caption generation

Using photo metadata and image recognition, the AI suggests captions with dates, places, and context. "August 14 — Lake Como." You edit, add your own words, or leave them as-is.

What AI can't do (and why that's fine)

We believe in being honest about what AI does well and where it needs a human hand. Our AI photo book maker is powerful, but it's not perfect — and that's by design.

  • It doesn't know the inside joke. The AI can't tell that the blurry photo of your friend mid-laugh is actually the most important shot of the trip. That's why every Remeet book goes through human review — and why you can always swap, add, or remove photos.
  • It can't read your mind about emphasis. Maybe the wedding matters more than the vacation. Maybe you want five pages on the puppy and one on the holiday party. You tell us, we adjust.
  • It's a starting point, not a final answer. Think of the AI as a brilliant first draft. You refine it. The combination of machine intelligence and your personal knowledge is what makes the book feel truly yours.
