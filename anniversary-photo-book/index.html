Why an anniversary photo book

A timeline of your relationship

Dating, engagement, the wedding, first apartment, first pet, first kid, the vacations, the renovations, the quiet Tuesday nights. An anniversary book organizes the chaos of years into a story that flows — chapter by chapter, year by year.

A milestone marker

Anniversaries deserve more than dinner and flowers. A photo book captures where you've been and how far you've come. Whether it's year one or year fifty, holding the whole journey in your hands makes the milestone real.

A gift for your spouse

Surprise your partner with a book of your life together. It's the kind of gift that makes someone stop talking mid-sentence and just flip pages. Personal, thoughtful, and impossible to buy in a store.

A gift for your parents' anniversary

Gather photos from siblings, dig through the family albums, and give your parents something they'd never make for themselves. A book of their decades together, designed with the care their story deserves.

Anniversary books by milestone

1st anniversary: paper

There's a reason the traditional first anniversary gift is paper. One year of marriage, one beautiful book. Include your wedding day, honeymoon, the first apartment, the first holidays as a married couple. It's a small window of time, but the photos are fresh and the emotions are vivid. Start the tradition now — add pages every year.

10th anniversary: a decade together

Ten years is when you start to forget things. Not the big moments, but the little ones — the road trip where the car broke down, the Halloween costumes, the Sunday mornings. A decade of photos fills a substantial book with real depth. You'll rediscover photos you forgot you took.

25th anniversary: silver

A quarter century of life together. Kids who were toddlers are now adults. Houses have changed. Haircuts have changed. But the core of the story is the same two people choosing each other, year after year. A 25th anniversary book is part love story, part family history.

50th anniversary: gold

Fifty years of marriage is extraordinary. The photos span from black-and-white to digital, from bell-bottoms to today. A golden anniversary book is often a gift from children or grandchildren — a tribute to the couple who built the family. We'll handle the mix of photo formats, eras, and quality levels with care.

Frequently asked questions

How many years of photos can I include in one anniversary book?

As many as you want. Whether you're celebrating 1 year or 50, we'll design the book to fit your timeline. Most books run 80-120 pages, but we can go longer for couples with decades of memories. We organize by year or era so the story flows naturally from beginning to present day.

Can I include our wedding photos?

Absolutely. Wedding photos are often the perfect opening chapter. If your wedding photos are only in print form (not digital), you can scan them with your phone camera or a scanner app and send those to us. We'll clean them up and integrate them seamlessly with your digital photos.

Is an anniversary photo book a good gift for my parents' anniversary?

It's one of the most meaningful gifts you can give. Gather photos from siblings, family albums, and your own collection. Parents rarely expect their children to make something like this, and the reaction is almost always tears. We've designed books for 25th, 40th, and 50th anniversaries that became the centerpiece of the celebration.

How do I get old pre-digital photos into the book?

Use your phone camera or a free scanner app like Google PhotoScan to photograph printed photos, slides, or album pages. The quality is usually excellent. You can also mail us physical photos and we'll scan them professionally and return them safely. We work with whatever you have — even slightly faded or imperfect originals.

How far in advance should I order for our anniversary?

We recommend starting at least 2-3 weeks before your anniversary date. We design the book in 72 hours, then you review and approve, and printing plus shipping takes 7-10 business days. If you're short on time, order the Digital Book first — it's ready instantly — and the printed copy ships later.

Can both partners contribute photos?

Yes, and we encourage it. Each partner has their own perspective and their own favorite moments. You can both share photos separately — from your phone, Google Photos, iCloud, or any cloud album — and we'll weave them together into one cohesive story. It's also a great way to surprise each other with forgotten photos.
