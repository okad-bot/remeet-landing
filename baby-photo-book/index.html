How it works

Share everything

Send your Google Photos album or camera roll. No sorting, no picking favorites. We'll find the strongest moments from hundreds or thousands of photos.

We design the book

Our designers curate your best photos, remove duplicates, organize chapters (first days, first discoveries, growing up), and create an editorial-quality layout.

Review your proof

Get a complete digital proof in 72 hours. Want changes? Just tell us: "Add more bath time photos" or "Use the other cover shot."

Print and keep

Approve your proof and we'll print a premium hardcover book with archival-quality paper. Shipping from $7.60. Extra copies for grandparents from $79.

What's inside a Remeet baby book

  • Curated photo selection — We pick the 100-150 strongest photos from your library
  • Chapter structure — "The day you arrived," "First discoveries," "Our people," "Growing up"
  • Captions — Specific, personal captions written to your child: "You held this toy everywhere for three months"
  • Editorial design — Magazine-quality layouts, not basic grids. Thoughtful pacing and white space.
  • 60-80+ pages — Enough room to tell the real story, not just the highlights

Why parents choose Remeet

You don't have time to design a photo book. Between sleep regressions and feeding schedules, the last thing you need is a 5-hour design project. Remeet takes 5 minutes of your time — share your photos and we handle the rest.

Your baby's first year won't wait. The longer you put it off, the harder it gets. Details fade. You forget which month that photo was from. Start now while the memories are fresh.
