Why grandparents love photo books

They ask you to send photos. You text a few, they save them to a folder they can't find later. Or you share a Google Photos link and they can't figure out how to scroll through it on their tablet.

A printed photo book solves all of that. It's right there on the coffee table. No passwords, no apps, no pinching to zoom. Just beautiful photos of the people they love most, organized into a story they can revisit any time they want.

And when friends come over? That book is coming off the shelf within five minutes.

What makes a great grandparent photo book

A physical book they can hold

Not a digital link. Not a slideshow. A real, hardcover book with thick pages that feels like it matters. Because it does.

Easy-to-read captions

We use generous font sizes and clear typography. Every caption is readable without squinting. If they need larger text, we adjust.

Chapters that tell a story

"A year with the grandkids," "Summer at the lake," "First day of school." We organize photos into chapters that give the book structure and meaning.

An annual tradition

Many families order a new book each year. Christmas, birthdays, or just because. A growing collection on Grandma's bookshelf.

How it works

  1. Share your photos — Send us photos from your phone, Google Photos, or iCloud. School photos, holidays, everyday moments — the more the better.
  2. We design the book — Our team curates the best photos, organizes them into chapters, adds captions, and creates a beautiful layout. Ready in 72 hours.
  3. Review and approve — See the full digital proof. Request changes — "Add the photo from Easter" or "Make the captions larger." Unlimited revisions.
  4. We print and ship — Premium hardcover, archival paper. Ships free anywhere in the US, directly to their door if you'd like.

Frequently asked questions

Can I order copies for multiple grandparents?

Yes. Order as many copies as you need. Additional copies are $179 each, with discounts starting at 2 copies. We can ship each copy to a different address.

Can you ship directly to my grandparents?

Absolutely. Just provide their address at checkout. The book arrives in a kraft gift box with tissue wrapping, ready to open. No additional wrapping needed.

Is the text large enough for elderly readers?

Our designers use generous font sizes for captions and chapter titles. If you'd like extra-large text, just let us know during the review phase and we'll adjust the layout.

What photos should I include?

Send everything — grandkids at school, holidays, FaceTime screenshots, visits to grandma's house, everyday moments. The more you share, the richer the story. We curate the best ones.

How often should I make a photo book for grandparents?

Many families make it an annual tradition — a new book each year at Christmas or for birthdays. Some do one per grandchild. There's no wrong cadence.

Can grandparents request changes to the book?

The person who orders the book manages the review process. But you can absolutely share the proof with grandparents and relay their feedback to us. Revisions are unlimited before printing.
