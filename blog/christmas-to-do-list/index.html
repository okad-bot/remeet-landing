Your Christmas photo book to-do list

November 2026 · 6 min read
Written by Olga Miller, founder of ReMeet

A photo book is one of the best Christmas gifts you can give. It's personal, it's permanent, and it doesn't end up in a drawer by February. But timing matters. Photo books take time to design, review, print, and ship. Miss a deadline and you're wrapping an IOU instead of a book.

Here's the exact checklist so that doesn't happen.

The key difference: with DIY services like Shutterfly and Mixbook, you need to finish the design before you order. That means adding four to eight hours of design time to the schedule. With ReMeet, you just share a photo link and the design is handled for you.

Week-by-week checklist

Early November: gather and decide

  • Decide who you're making a book for. Grandparents? Your partner? Your kids?
  • Gather photos into one album. Google Photos, iCloud, Dropbox, or a folder on your computer all work.
  • Ask family members to share their photos too. The best books have photos from multiple perspectives.
  • Don't worry about sorting or picking favorites yet. Just get everything into one place.

Mid November: choose a service and order

  • Pick your service. If you want done-for-you, share the link with ReMeet by December 1 (or November 25 for premium layflat).
  • If you're going DIY, start designing now. Don't wait. The editor always takes longer than you think.
  • Set a calendar reminder for your approval deadline so the proof doesn't sit in your inbox.

Late November: review and revise

  • Review your proof carefully. Check names in captions, photo order, and cropping.
  • Request any changes. With ReMeet, two rounds of edits are included.
  • If you're doing DIY, this is your last chance to fix layout issues before the order deadline.

Early December: approve and print

  • Approve the final design by December 5 (for classic hardcover).
  • Order the print. Confirm your shipping address.
  • If you're making books for multiple people, order all copies at once.

Mid December: receive and wrap

  • Books arrive between December 15 and 18 for standard shipping within the US.
  • Inspect the book when it arrives. Check for printing issues.
  • Wrap it up. You're done.

If you're using a DIY editor

You need to add four to eight hours of design time to the schedule. That's not an exaggeration. Here's what the work looks like:

  • Upload photos (20-30 minutes for a large album)
  • Sort through and pick which photos to include (30-60 minutes)
  • Choose a template and arrange photos on each page (1-2 hours)
  • Fix cropping issues where heads and feet get cut off (30-60 minutes)
  • Add text, captions, or titles (20-30 minutes)
  • Review every page and make adjustments (30-60 minutes)
  • Go back and fix the things you missed the first time (30 minutes)

Start earlier than you think you need to. Many people begin a DIY photo book with the best intentions and then run out of time or energy halfway through. Starting in early November gives you buffer.

Gift ideas by recipient

Not sure what kind of book to make? Here are some ideas based on who it's for:

  • Grandparents. A year of grandkid photos, organized by season. This is consistently the most popular Christmas photo book gift. See our full guide: photo book gifts for grandparents.
  • New parents. A baby's first year book covering the first months. If the baby was born this year, this is the perfect gift.
  • Your partner. A book covering the year you've had together. Trips, dinners, lazy Sundays, the small moments.
  • Your family. A family yearbook that everyone can flip through on Christmas morning.
  • Yourself. There's no rule that says photo books have to be gifts. Make one for your own shelf.

What if you've already missed the deadline?

It's mid-December and you just found this article. Here are your options:

  • Digital PDF. Order a ReMeet digital book by December 20. You'll have the proof by December 23. Share it digitally on Christmas Day, then order the printed version to arrive in January.
  • Same-day pickup. Walgreens offers same-day photo book printing. The quality is basic, but it's something physical under the tree. See our last-minute photo book guide.
  • Promise card. Write a card that says the photo book is on its way. Order the ReMeet proof, let the recipient know it's coming. The anticipation is part of the gift.

The worst option is doing nothing because you think it's too late. It's not. A photo book that arrives a week after Christmas is still a meaningful gift.
