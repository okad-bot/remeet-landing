You have thousands of photos on your phone. You've been meaning to turn them into a book for years. You even opened Shutterfly once, spent forty minutes dragging images into a grid, realized you were on page four of eighty, and closed the tab.

That's the normal experience. Most photo book projects get abandoned before they're finished. The problem isn't that people don't care about their photos. It's that sorting, selecting, and arranging thousands of images into a coherent book takes 4 to 8 hours. Most people don't have that time.

I built ReMeet for exactly this. You share a link to your album. I design the book. That's the whole thing.

What's included for $29

  • Photo selection. I go through your entire album and pick the shots that tell the story best.
  • Duplicate removal. Near-identical photos, blurry frames, screenshots, and parking-sign photos stay out.
  • Sorting by date. Your book reads chronologically, month by month. Trips and birthdays get their own sections.
  • Hand cropping. Every photo cropped individually. No cut-off faces, no stray fingers at the edges.
  • Page-by-page design. Custom layouts with real attention to spacing, rhythm, and white space. Not a template grid.
  • Captions. Short, natural notes based on what's in your photos. "August, the beach house." You can change any of them.
  • Quality check. I review every page before you see it. No repeats, nothing cut off, no empty pages.
  • Two rounds of edits. Tell me what to change in plain words. I revise until you're happy.
  • Print-ready PDF. Download it, share it on a tablet, or order a printed copy.

How one $29 book became a series of three

A client ordered a 30-page book of a family trip. When she saw the proof, she realized the photos told a bigger story than one book could hold. The vacation was just the latest chapter - there were the kids' birthdays, a house renovation, and a surprise 40th party in the same year.

She upgraded to three books: one for each part of the year. I redesigned the original trip book as one volume, then built two more from the same album. All three were printed as premium layflat hardcovers and went to different family members as gifts.

What started as a quick $29 book became a small family library. That's the thing about photo books - once you see your photos designed properly, you notice how many more stories are sitting in your camera roll.

Frequently asked questions

What if I don't like the design?

If you don't love the first draft and don't want changes, you get a full refund. If you like the direction but want changes, two rounds of edits are included and each extra round is $25. Once you ask for edits, the next version is a second draft and the refund is off. Just tell me the page number and what you want different.

Can I request a specific style?

Yes. When you place your order, tell me what you're looking for: clean and minimal, playful and colorful, editorial with lots of white space, or anything else. I design every book individually, so your style preference shapes the layouts, fonts, and spacing from the start.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds of revisions are included. In each round, you can change as many pages as you want. Swap photos, adjust captions, move images to different pages, remove a spread. Just describe what you want in plain words. Nothing prints until you approve.

How is this different from Shutterfly or Mixbook?

Shutterfly and Mixbook give you an editor where you design the book yourself: pick photos, choose layouts, arrange pages, write captions. ReMeet does all of that for you. You share an album link, and I deliver a finished book. The difference is ordering furniture versus assembling flatpack yourself.

Do I need to sort my photos first?

No. Send me your entire album, up to 5,000 photos. I sort them by date, remove duplicates and blurry shots, and pick the best ones for the book. The messier your camera roll, the more time I save you. Here's how to share a Google Photos album.
