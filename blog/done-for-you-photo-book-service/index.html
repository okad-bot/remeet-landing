Done-For-You Photo Book: We Sort, Crop, Design, and Check Every Page

November 2026 · 10 min read

You have 3,000 photos on your phone. You have been meaning to turn them into a book for two years. You even opened Shutterfly once, spent forty minutes dragging images into a grid, realized you were on page four of eighty, and closed the tab.

That is the normal experience. The photo book industry's own data shows that roughly 83% of photo book projects get abandoned before they are finished. The problem is not that people do not care about their photos. The problem is that sorting, selecting, cropping, and arranging thousands of images into a coherent book is genuinely hard work. It takes hours, and most people do not have hours to spare.

Remeet exists because of that gap. We are not a photo book editor. We do not give you a tool and wish you luck. We take your photos and give you back a finished book. The difference is like ordering a piece of furniture versus buying a flatpack from IKEA and spending your Saturday with an Allen key.

What "done for you" actually means

When we say we handle everything, we mean every single step between your photos and a finished book. Here is the full process, in order.

1. You share a link

Send us a Google Photos album, an iCloud shared album, a Dropbox folder, or any link where your photos live. It does not matter how many there are. Fifty photos or five thousand, the process is the same. You do not need to organize anything first. Just share the link.

2. We go through every photo

Our team reviews every single image in your album. We are looking for the ones that tell your story best: the expressions, the moments, the places that matter. If you have 2,000 photos from a year, we might select 80 to 120 for the book. For a deeper look at how our selection process works, see how AI selects your best photos.

3. We remove the noise

Duplicate shots, blurry frames, accidental screenshots, photos of receipts and parking signs: they all stay out. Near-identical photos from the same moment get narrowed down to the best one. This step alone can cut an album of 3,000 images down to a manageable set of a few hundred.

4. We sort by date and group by events

Your book reads like your life did. Photos are arranged chronologically, month by month. Trips, holidays, birthdays, and big moments get their own chapter with a title page, so they are easy to find when you flip through the book later. Our article on how AI photo books work explains how we use metadata and visual analysis to group your photos accurately.

5. We crop every photo by hand

This is the step that most automated services skip entirely. We crop each photo individually so that nobody gets cut off at the edge of the page. Sideways shots get rotated. Small faces in a group photo get a closer crop so you can actually see them. Stray fingers and clutter at the edges get trimmed. Every face stays in frame, with room above their heads.

6. We design clean layouts

This is not a template with photos dropped into slots. We design each page with real attention to balance, rhythm, and white space. Full-page photos alternate with multi-image spreads. Quiet pages follow busy ones. The result feels more like a magazine than a scrapbook. For the full list of everything our design process covers, see what we handle.

7. We write captions in your voice

We draft short, handwritten-style notes based on what is in your photos. A trip to the coast gets a simple "August, the beach house." A birthday party might get "Turning three. Chocolate everywhere." The captions are gentle and personal. Change any of them, or keep them as they are.

8. We check every page

Before you see anything, we review every page of the book ourselves. No repeated photos. Nothing cut off. No text covering a face. No pages that feel empty or overcrowded. This quality check catches the things that automated layout tools miss because algorithms do not have eyes the way a human designer does.

9. You review and request changes

We send you a proof. You flip through every page and tell us what to change. Swap a photo, adjust a caption, move a picture to a different page. Just say the page number and what you want. Revisions are included in the price, and we keep going until you love every page.

10. Delivered, digitally or printed

Once you approve, we deliver a high-resolution PDF you can share with family or view on any screen. If you want a printed hardcover, we handle that too: professional printing, quality paper, and shipping to your door.

Why we built this

Most photo book services assume you want to design your own book. They give you an editor with templates, fonts, backgrounds, stickers, and dozens of layout options. The editor is the product.

For some people, that is great. They enjoy the creative process. They have the time. They know which photos to pick and how to arrange them.

But for most people, the editor is the obstacle. The reason 83% of photo book projects never get finished is not that the software is hard to use. It is that the real work happens before you even open the editor: going through thousands of photos, deciding which ones matter, figuring out how to tell the story, and arranging everything so it looks good. That part takes four to eight hours, and there is no shortcut.

We built Remeet to do that part. Not to give you a faster editor, but to remove the need for one entirely. You share your album, and we turn it into a book. That is the whole product.
