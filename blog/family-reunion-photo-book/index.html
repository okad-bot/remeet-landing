Family Reunion Photo Book: One Book for Every Branch

Updated Nov 2026
Olga, designer at ReMeet, 10 years in editorial design

Collect photos from every family into a shared Google Photos album. I pick the best shots, organize by family and activity, add names, and design a book you can print for every household. One design fee ($29 or $79), then $69 per printed copy. Ship to different addresses, US and Canada shipping included.

Step 1: Collect photos from everyone

This is the hardest part. Here's what works:

  1. Before the reunion. Create a shared Google Photos album. Name it "Smith Family Reunion 2026" (or whatever fits). Share the link with the family group chat.
  2. During the reunion. Remind people to add photos to the shared album. Designate one person as the photographer for group shots.
  3. After the reunion. Give everyone two weeks to upload their photos. Send one reminder. After the deadline, share the album link with me.

If some family members use iCloud instead of Google Photos, they can share an iCloud link separately. I'll pull from multiple sources.

Step 2: I design the book

I organize reunion books in one of two ways, depending on what fits better:

By family branch. Each family gets their own section. The Smith side, the Johnson side, the cousins. Group photos open each section, candids fill it out. This works well when each branch has distinct moments.

By activity. Arrival and setup, the barbecue, games and sports, the group photo, the kids' table, the goodbye. This works when everyone was mixed together the whole time.

Either way, I include:

  • A cover with the family name and year
  • The big group photo on the first spread
  • Names under group shots (you send me a key)
  • Candid action shots mixed with posed photos
  • A family tree page or attendee list (optional)
  • Captions with names, ages, and context for future generations

Why names matter

Label the group shots. In 20 years, the youngest kids in the photo won't recognize everyone. Send me a quick note: "Front row left to right: Aunt Linda, Uncle Dave, Grandma Rose..." I'll add names as captions.

This is the difference between a book that gets looked at once and a book that gets pulled off the shelf every Thanksgiving.

Printing copies for each household

After you approve the design, tell me how many copies and where to ship them. Each printed hardcover is $69, premium layflat from $129. I handle printing and shipping directly to each address.

Some families split the cost evenly. Others have one person cover the design fee and each family pays for their own copy. The PDF is also yours to share digitally with anyone who wants it.

Frequently asked questions

How do we collect photos from everyone?

Create a shared Google Photos album and send the link to every family. Anyone with the link can add photos. Set a deadline (two weeks after the reunion works well). One person collects stragglers and shares the final album with me.

How much does a reunion book cost for 5 families?

For a 30-page book with 5 printed copies: $29 design + 5 x $69 printing = $374 total. That's about $75 per family if you split the cost. US and Canada shipping is included.

Can you ship copies to different addresses?

Yes. I can ship each copy to a different address. US and Canada shipping is included. Europe is +$15 per book. Just send me the list of addresses when you order.

Should I label who is in each group photo?

Yes, please. Send a quick note identifying people in the larger group shots. I'll add names as captions. This makes the book useful for future generations who won't recognize everyone.

What if the reunion happens every year?

Annual reunion books make a great tradition. After the first book, I save your design style and preferences. Each year takes the same amount of time and cost, but the books get better because you know the process.
