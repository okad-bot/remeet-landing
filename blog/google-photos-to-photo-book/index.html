How to share a Google Photos album

You don't need to download anything. Here's the process, step by step.

  1. Open Google Photos on your phone or computer.
  2. Go to the album you want to turn into a book. If you don't have one, create it: tap the + icon, select "Album", and add photos by date range or by picking them manually.
  3. Tap the share icon (the arrow pointing up, or the three dots menu on desktop).
  4. Choose "Create link" or "Get link". This generates a view-only link anyone with the URL can see.
  5. Copy the link and paste it into the ReMeet order form. That's it.

The whole thing takes about two minutes. You don't share your Google password. You don't download files. You don't re-upload anything. I see only the album you shared, nothing else in your account.

Tip: Don't over-curate. It's tempting to pre-select your favorites, but I often find photos you'd skip. The candid shot where nobody is posing. The detail of a table setting. The blurry action photo that works as a full-bleed spread. When in doubt, share more, not less.

What happens after you share the link

Here's what I do with your photos once I have the link.

  • Duplicates removed. Those 14 nearly identical shots of the same moment get narrowed down to the strongest one.
  • Blurry and accidental shots filtered. Screenshots, dark photos, and that pocket shot of your shoe are gone.
  • Grouped by time and place. Photos are organized into chapters. A trip to the coast. A birthday party. A quiet Sunday at home. The book follows the story of your year.
  • Designed page by page. Each spread gets its own layout. Full-bleed landscapes, close-ups paired with wide shots, white space where the page needs to breathe. No grids. No templates.
  • Captions added. Based on your notes and photo metadata, I add dates, locations, and short captions. If you send a few lines about what mattered that year, they go in too.

First proof arrives in 72 hours. You review it, ask for changes in plain words ("swap the cover photo", "add more beach shots", "move the birthday to page 10"), and I revise it. Two rounds of changes are included.

Frequently asked questions

Do you need my Google password?

No. You share a Google Photos album link, which gives view-only access to that specific album. I never see your account, your other albums, or anything outside what you share. You can revoke access anytime by turning off the shared link.

What if the album has 3,000 photos?

That's fine. ReMeet accepts up to 5,000 photos per book. Software sorts and groups them first, then I go through and pick the strongest shots. More photos means more to choose from, so bigger albums usually produce better books.

Can I add videos?

Yes. If your album includes videos, I can pull still frames from the best moments. First steps, birthday candles, travel footage where you filmed instead of taking a photo. Video frame extraction is available as an add-on (+$50).

What if I want to add photos from my iPhone too?

You can share multiple links. A Google Photos album, an iCloud link, a Dropbox folder. I combine everything into one book. Just paste all the links when you order.

How many photos do I need for a 30-page book?

About 50 to 100 photos is a good starting point. For 100 pages, 200 to 500 works well. But you don't need to count. Send the whole album and I'll work with what's there.
