Why honeymoon photos are perfect for a book

Most trips produce a mix of landscapes, food, selfies, and candid moments. A honeymoon has all of that, plus the emotional weight of a first trip together as a married couple. That variety is exactly what makes a good photo book. Every page looks different. Every spread tells a different part of the story.

The problem is that honeymoon albums are usually huge. You come back with 800, 1,500, sometimes 3,000 photos on two phones. Turning that into a book yourself means hours of sorting, deciding, cropping, and arranging. Most people don't finish. The photos stay in the cloud, unsorted, slowly fading from memory.

That's what ReMeet solves. You share the album. I do the rest.

What to include beyond the obvious

The big moments are easy: the sunset, the beach, the view from the balcony. But the best honeymoon books also have the in-between shots that you almost delete:

  • The airport selfie at 5 a.m., still in your wedding hair
  • The hotel room when you first walked in
  • The street food you ate standing up
  • The local market where you bought something silly
  • The rainy afternoon when you stayed in and ordered room service
  • Screenshots of flight confirmations, hotel bookings, restaurant maps

Don't delete any of these. Send me everything. I pick what works best for the layout and skip the rest. The messy, unfiltered camera roll is the best source material.

How the book gets organized

I sort your photos by date and GPS data, then group them into chapters. A typical honeymoon book might look like this:

ChapterWhat goes in
Day 1 - ArrivalAirport, hotel check-in, first dinner, neighborhood walk
Day 2-3 - Beach townBeach shots, snorkeling, cocktails, sunset
Day 4 - Road tripCar rental, roadside stops, local market
Day 5-6 - MountainsHiking, cabin, campfire, panoramic views
Last day - Going homePacking, last breakfast, airport, the seat with the view

If you visited multiple cities, each city becomes a section. Full-bleed landscape spreads break up the chapters. Close-up details - a hand holding a coffee cup, a street sign, a ticket stub - add texture between the big shots.

Combining two phones

You both took photos. That's normal. Here's how to get them into one book:

  1. Create a shared Google Photos album for the trip
  2. Both add your photos to it
  3. Share the album link with me

Or just send me two separate links. I merge everything and sort by timestamp, so the story flows in order regardless of which phone took the shot. Duplicates (you both took the same sunset) get caught by the AI and I keep the sharper one.

Making it an anniversary tradition

Some couples order a new book every year. The honeymoon book becomes volume one. Year two: a weekend trip, a hike, a lazy Saturday morning. Year three: a baby on the way, the new apartment, Thanksgiving at the in-laws.

Each book is 30 pages at $29. After five years, you have a shelf of five slim books that tell the whole story. It's cheaper and more personal than one large book you try to update.

If you'd rather put the whole year into one volume, the travel photo book option works for that too.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine photos from two phones?

Yes. Create one shared Google Photos album and have both of you add your photos to it. Or send two separate links. I merge everything and sort by date, so the timeline stays clean regardless of which phone took the shot.

What if we took 2,000 photos on the trip?

Send them all. I accept up to 5,000 photos per book. The AI sorts through duplicates, blurry shots, and near-identical angles, then I pick the best ones for the final layout. More photos means more to choose from.

Can I add the wedding photos too?

Yes. You can combine the wedding and the honeymoon into one book (the 100-page option at $79 gives plenty of room), or keep them as two separate books. If the events feel like different chapters, two 30-page books at $29 each often work better than one large one.

Do you ship internationally?

Printed books ship to the US, Canada and Europe. US and Canada shipping is included, Europe is +$15. The designed PDF works worldwide. You can also print it at any local shop.

Can I request a specific style?

Yes. Tell me what you're after: editorial with lots of white space, warm and cozy, modern and clean. I design every book individually, so your preference shapes the layouts, fonts, and spacing from the start.
