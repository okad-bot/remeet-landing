How Long Does It Take to Make a Photo Book?

Updated Nov 2026
Olga, designer at ReMeet, 10 years in editorial design

With ReMeet, you spend about 5 minutes sharing a link to your photos. I send a designed proof in 72 hours. Revisions take 24 hours each. The whole process from sharing photos to holding a printed book takes about 2-3 weeks. If you design it yourself, expect 5-12 hours of your own time before printing.

DIY timeline: the honest version

What affects speed

  • Number of photos. 200 photos take me the same 72 hours as 2,000. More photos means more to sort, but the design time stays about the same. For DIY, more photos means more time selecting and arranging.
  • Revisions. Most books need one revision round (24 hours). A second round is included. If you're happy with the first draft, we're done on day 3.
  • Digital vs print. If you only need the PDF, the book is done in about 5 days. Printing adds 1-2 weeks.
  • Shipping destination. US and Canada ship in 3-7 business days (included). Europe takes 5-10 days and costs +$15.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a ReMeet photo book take?

First draft in 72 hours from when you share your photos. Each revision round takes about 24 hours. The designed PDF is usually done within 5 days. Printing and shipping adds 5-10 business days.

How long does it take to make a photo book yourself?

Most people spend 5-12 hours over several sessions. Sorting and selecting photos takes the longest (2-4 hours), followed by designing the layout (2-5 hours), then writing captions and reviewing (1-3 hours). Many DIY projects stall because people run out of time mid-design.

Can I get a photo book done in one day?

For DIY, Chatbooks can auto-generate a simple book in minutes. For a designed book, ReMeet delivers a first draft in 72 hours. Same-day isn't possible for a custom-designed book, but a designed digital PDF can be done in under a week.

What's the deadline for a Christmas photo book?

For printed delivery to the US, Canada, or Europe by Christmas 2026, order printing by December 1. For a designed PDF gift (no physical printing), order by December 20.

How long does printing and shipping take?

Printing takes 3-5 business days. Shipping to the US and Canada takes 3-7 business days (included in the price). Europe adds 5-10 business days and costs +$15. Total from approval to delivery: about 1-2 weeks.
