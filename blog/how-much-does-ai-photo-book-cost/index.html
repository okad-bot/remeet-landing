How printing works

Printing is a separate step. After I send you the proof and you approve it, you can:

  1. Order a printed copy through ReMeet. Hardcover from $69, premium layflat from $129. I handle the printing and ship it to you. US and Canada shipping included, Europe +$15.
  2. Print the PDF yourself. Take it to FedEx Office, Staples, or a local print shop. The file is 300 DPI with bleed marks. Costs vary, usually $15-$40 for a bound book.
  3. Keep the digital version. Share it on a tablet, email it to family, or save it for later.

You are never locked into printing through me. The PDF is yours.

Hidden costs to watch for

  • Extra pages. Mixbook charges $1-$3 per extra page. Artifact Uprising charges $2-$4. At ReMeet, you pick 30 or 100 pages upfront, no per-page charges.
  • Shipping. Most services charge $8-$15 for standard shipping. ReMeet includes US and Canada shipping.
  • Coupon dependence. Shutterfly and Snapfish nearly always run 40-60% off sales. Their "full price" is not what anyone actually pays. If you see a $60 Shutterfly book, expect to pay $25-$35 with a coupon.
  • Rush delivery. Expedited shipping adds $10-$25 at most services. Plan ahead for gifts.
  • Premium add-ons. Dust jackets, foil stamping, custom covers, and gift boxes add $10-$40 at Artifact Uprising and Blurb.
  • Time. Your time is a real cost. A "free" design tool that takes 6 hours is not free.

Frequently asked questions

Is printing included in the $29?

No. The $29 is the design fee for a print-ready PDF up to 30 pages. Printing is a separate step that starts at $69 for a hardcover, only after you approve the design. You can also take the PDF to any local print shop.

How much does it cost to get 3 printed copies?

For a 30-page book: $29 design + $69 first print + $69 second copy + $69 third copy = $236 total. US and Canada shipping is included. Europe is +$15 per book.

What about shipping to Europe?

Shipping to the US and Canada is included in the print price. Europe is +$15 per book. The designed PDF is delivered digitally and works worldwide.

What is the cheapest way to get a photo book?

The cheapest printed photo book is Google Photos at around $15 for a softcover, but you design it yourself. The cheapest designed photo book is ReMeet at $29 for a 30-page PDF where I do all the work.

Can I print the PDF at a local shop instead?

Yes. The PDF is print-ready at 300 DPI with bleed marks. FedEx Office, Staples, and most local print shops can bind it as a hardcover or softcover. Costs vary by shop but typically run $15-$40.
