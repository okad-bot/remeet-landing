Last-Minute Christmas Photo Gift: Order by Dec 15, Get It by Dec 24

November 2026 · 7 min read

It is December and you still don't have a gift for that person who says they don't want anything. Or for the parent who already has everything. Or for the grandparent who lives across the country and just wants to feel close to the family.

Here is the good news: you can order a professionally designed photo book right now, and it will arrive before Christmas. No craft supplies. No design skills. No hours lost in a drag-and-drop editor. You share the photos, someone else makes the book, and you give a gift that will sit on a shelf for decades.

The deadline is December 15. Here is exactly how the timeline works and what you need to do.

The deadline

Order by December 15 to receive your printed book by December 24. Remeet designs your book within 72 hours. After you approve the proof, printing and shipping take 4-5 business days. That puts delivery at December 23-24 with standard shipping.

What you need: less than you think

You do not need to spend an evening curating, editing, or organizing. Here is the bare minimum:

  • 30-60 photos. They are already on your phone. Don't overthink it — your favorites from the year are enough.
  • A way to share them. Google Photos, iCloud, Dropbox, or a Google Drive link. Whatever is easiest for you.
  • A rough idea of the story. "Our year as a family." "Grandkids highlights for Grandma." "Our first year together." One sentence is enough — the designer fills in the rest.

That is it. No captions required (though you can add them if you want). No specific order required. No photo editing needed.

The 10-minute photo selection method

If scrolling through a year of photos feels overwhelming, use this method. It works every time:

  1. Open your camera roll. Go to January.
  2. Swipe through each month. Tap the heart/favorite on the 4-5 best photos from that month. Don't agonize — if you pause on a photo, it is probably worth including.
  3. Repeat for all 12 months. You will end up with 48-60 photos in about 10 minutes.
  4. Share your favorites folder. Done.

The result is a naturally chronological collection that captures the year without requiring you to remember every event or trip. Your phone already did the remembering for you.

Don't wait for perfect

The most common mistake is spending too long choosing photos. A book with 40 good photos finished on time beats a book with 80 perfect photos that arrives on December 27. Share what you have. The designer will make it look great.

Three book ideas you can order tonight

Not sure what kind of book to make? These three concepts work for almost anyone and take minutes to set up.

1. Year in review

Pick your favorite moments from each month of 2026. Trips, birthdays, random Tuesday dinners, the dog in the snow, the garden in summer. This is the most universal option and works for spouses, parents, grandparents, or even yourself. For more ideas on structuring a year-end book, see our guide to creating a year-in-review photo book.

2. Relationship highlights

For a partner or a close friend: pull the photos from your shared experiences this year. Date nights, weekend trips, lazy mornings, concerts, the meal you cooked together that actually turned out well. This works especially well for couples, parent-child relationships, or lifelong friends.

3. Grandparent book

Grandparents consistently say photo books are the best gift they receive. Fill it with photos of the grandkids — school events, silly faces, sports games, holiday mornings, FaceTime screenshots. Include a few with the grandparent in the frame too. If you are looking for more ideas for grandparent gifts, our Christmas photo book for grandparents guide covers exactly what to include.

Why this beats a last-minute Amazon order

We have all been there. December 22, panic-scrolling Amazon for something with next-day delivery. You end up with a candle, a pair of socks, or a gadget that says "I ran out of time."

A photo book says the opposite. It says: I went through our memories. I thought about what matters to you. I made something that no one else could give you. The fact that it arrives just in time makes the story better, not worse.

For more than one person? Order multiple copies

If you are making a family yearbook or a grandparent book, you might want copies for multiple people. The design is done once — additional printed copies just add the printing cost. One design, three grandparents, three gifts. Done.

This also works for siblings. Make one family book and give a copy to each sibling as a shared Christmas gift from the whole family. Everyone gets the same memories, beautifully printed.

What if I miss the December 15 deadline?

If you order after December 15, you may not receive the physical book by Christmas Eve. But you have options:

  • Gift the PDF proof. You will receive the digital proof within 72 hours of ordering. Print the cover page, put it in a card, and give it on Christmas Day with a note that the real book is on its way.
  • Order with expedited shipping. Depending on your location, expedited options may extend the deadline by a few days. Email us and we will work out the fastest route.
  • Make it a New Year's gift instead. A year-in-review photo book makes even more sense as a January 1 gift — you are closing out the year with all its best moments captured.
