The PDF option: how it works

A designed PDF is not a consolation prize. It is a finished, print-ready photo book file, designed page by page, the same way a printed copy would be. The difference is that you take it to a print shop yourself instead of waiting for shipping.

Here is the timeline:

  1. You order and share a link to your photos (Google Photos, iCloud, Dropbox).
  2. I design your book within 72 hours. Every page laid out by hand.
  3. You review the proof and request changes (two rounds included).
  4. You approve. I send the final print-ready PDF.
  5. You take the PDF to a copy shop and walk out with a printed book.

Total time on your end: about 15 minutes to share photos and review the proof. Everything else is handled for you.

Where to print a PDF the same day

Any shop that does color printing and binding can handle this. The most common options in the US and Canada:

  • FedEx Office. Color printing on heavy paper, coil or tape binding. Walk in, hand them the file, pick it up in a few hours.
  • Staples. Same idea. Some locations do same-day booklet printing if you order online in the morning.
  • Local print shops. Often faster and cheaper than chains. Call ahead to confirm they do color booklet printing.
  • Walmart or CVS photo center. Limited binding options, but they can print pages you assemble yourself.

The result will not look identical to a ReMeet hardcover. But it will be a real, physical book of your designed pages, and it will be in your hands the same day. For a detailed walkthrough, see how to print a photo book at a copy shop.

FAQ

What if I miss the December 1 deadline?

Order by December 20 instead. You get a designed, print-ready PDF within 72 hours. Take it to a FedEx Office, Staples, or any copy shop and have it printed and bound the same day. You can also share the digital book on a tablet Christmas morning.

Can I get a printed photo book by Christmas in Europe?

Shipping to Europe takes longer, so the safest option is a designed PDF. Order by December 20, and you will have a print-ready file you can take to any local print shop. If you want to try printed delivery, email hello@remeet.cc and I will check the fastest route for your country.

How fast is the design?

72 hours from when you share your photos. I pick the best shots, remove duplicates, and design every page by hand. Two rounds of revisions are included.

How many photos do I need?

For a 30-page book, 50 to 100 photos is a good range. For 100 pages, 200 to 500 works well. You can send more and I will pick the best ones.

Can I order copies for multiple people?

Yes. The design is done once. Each extra printed copy is $69. One design, three grandparents, three gifts.
