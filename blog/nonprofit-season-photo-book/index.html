Season Photo Book for Nonprofits, Teams and Schools

A season photo book captures one year of a team, school, church group, or nonprofit in a designed book. One person collects photos from members via a shared album, I design every page, and everyone orders copies at $69 each. Design from $29, proof in 72 hours.

Sports teams take thousands of photos in a season. Schools take more. Church groups, sailing clubs, dance companies, scout troops. The photos exist, but they live on twenty different phones and nobody's doing anything with them.

A season book solves that. One person collects the photos, sends them to a designer, and everyone who wants a copy gets one. The organization looks professional, the members get something they'll keep, and nobody had to learn layout software.

Who this works for

  • Youth sports teams. Soccer, swim, baseball, basketball. Season highlights, team photos, game days, awards ceremony. Parents love these.
  • Schools and PTAs. Class photos, field trips, spirit week, performances, graduation. One book per year, one per class, or one per grade.
  • Church groups. Mission trips, retreats, community events, holiday services. A book that documents the year for the congregation.
  • Nonprofits. Program highlights, volunteer events, fundraisers, impact stories. Use it in annual reports, donor gifts, or member appreciation.
  • Dance companies and clubs. Performances, rehearsals, recitals, group outings. A season record for dancers and families.

How it works

  1. Collect photos. Create a shared Google Photos album. Send the link to coaches, parents, volunteers, or anyone with photos. Set a deadline two to three weeks out.
  2. Share the album with me. One link, all the photos. I sort through everything, remove duplicates, and organize by event or date.
  3. Review the proof. You get a designed PDF within 72 hours. Tell me what to change in plain words. Two revision rounds are included.
  4. Order copies. Once the design is approved, collect orders from members and order printed copies. Hardcover from $69, layflat from $129.

What to include

The best season books tell the story of the year, not just the highlights. Here's what works:

  • Team and group photos. The formal photo plus the candid one where someone's making a face.
  • Action shots. Games, performances, events, volunteer work. The moments people showed up for.
  • Behind the scenes. Practice, prep, bus rides, group meals. The ordinary moments that define the experience.
  • Milestone events. Season opener, big wins, end-of-year ceremony, farewell events.
  • Text and stats. Season record, roster, event dates, award winners. Send these as a note and I place them in the design.

Collecting photos from groups

The biggest challenge with organization books is getting photos from many people. Here's what works:

  1. Create a Google Photos shared album at the start of the season, not the end.
  2. Share the link in your team group chat or parent email list.
  3. Remind people after key events: "Add your photos from Saturday's game."
  4. At season's end, share the album link with me. I handle the rest.

Starting the album early means people add photos as they go. Waiting until the end means chasing people for photos they may have already deleted.

Yes. Send your logo file and any text you want included. Season records, roster names, event dates, award winners. I place them into the layout alongside the photos. Send these as a text note when you share the album.
