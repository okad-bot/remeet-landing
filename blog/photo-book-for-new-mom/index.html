Photo Book Gift for a New Mom: What to Include

A photo book for a new mom captures pregnancy through the first months in one designed book. The partner or family collects photos, shares an album link, and I design every page. First proof in 72 hours, from $29. No sorting, no templates, no effort from the new mom.

New parents take hundreds of photos in the first weeks. Bump photos, hospital photos, tiny hands, first bath, 3am feeds. These photos live on a phone and get buried under newer ones. A year later, you're scrolling through thousands trying to find that one shot from the first night home.

A new-mom photo book takes all of those moments and puts them in order. Month by month, from pregnancy through the first months. It's the kind of gift a new mom doesn't have the time or energy to make for herself.

Who should make it

The best new-mom books are surprise gifts from someone who has access to the photos. Usually that means:

  • The partner. You were there for the pregnancy, the birth, and the early weeks. You have photos she's never even seen. This is your gift to make.
  • A grandparent or close family member. Ask the partner to share an album, add your own photos, and coordinate the surprise.
  • A friend who was at the baby shower. Combine shower photos with early baby photos the family shares with you.

The key is that the new mom doesn't have to do anything. She's already doing enough.

What to include

  • Pregnancy. Bump photos, ultrasound images, the nursery setup, the moment she found out.
  • Baby shower. Group photos, decorations, gifts, the cake. If a friend or family member photographed it, ask them for the album.
  • Hospital and birth. The drive to the hospital, waiting room moments, the first hold. Only include what the mom would want to see again.
  • First weeks. Coming home, first visitors, sleeping in the crib, feeding, baths. The ordinary moments that feel monumental.
  • Milestones. First smile, first time rolling over, first solid food. If you're making a full first-year book, include monthly milestones through month twelve.

When to give it

The timing depends on how many months you want to cover:

  • Three months. Enough for a 30-page book covering pregnancy through the first weeks. Good for a push present or early surprise.
  • Six months. A richer book with more milestones. Half a year of growth fills 30 to 50 pages easily.
  • First birthday. The full first year in one 100-page book. This is the most popular option. Give it on the baby's first birthday or Mother's Day.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to give a new mom a photo book?

Three to six months after the baby arrives. By then there are enough photos for a meaningful book, and the new mom has settled in enough to appreciate it. It also works well as a first birthday gift covering the whole first year.

Can I make this without the mom knowing?

Yes. Most new-mom books are surprise gifts from the partner. Collect photos from your own phone or ask family to send theirs. Put them in a Google Photos album and share the link with me. She never sees the album or proof.

How many photos should I include?

For a 30-page book, 80 to 150 photos is ideal. For 100 pages covering pregnancy through the first year, 300 to 500 photos works well. Send everything you have. I pick the best shots and organize by month.

Can I include ultrasound images?

Yes. Scan or photograph the ultrasound printout and add it to the album. It makes a great opening page. I also include pregnancy announcement photos, baby shower pictures, and hospital moments if you have them.

Can grandparents get their own copy?

Yes. Extra printed copies are $69 each. Many families order two or three copies so each set of grandparents has their own. US and Canada shipping is included, Europe is +$15.
