Why your Instagram photos deserve a book

Instagram isn't a backup. Accounts get hacked, suspended, or deleted. The app changes its algorithm, hides old posts, and compresses your photos. Ten years of memories sitting in a feed that might not exist next year.

A photo book is permanent. It doesn't need a password, a subscription, or a working server. It sits on your shelf and you can flip through it at the kitchen table.

Most people's Instagram is already curated. You chose those photos for a reason. They're the highlights of your year, your trips, your family. That makes them perfect source material for a book.

How to download your Instagram photos

  1. Open Instagram, go to Settings > Your activity > Download your information
  2. Select your account and choose JSON format (this includes captions and dates)
  3. Select Media to include photos and videos
  4. Choose high quality for the best resolution
  5. Hit Request download
  6. Wait for Instagram to email you a link (usually a few hours, sometimes up to 2 days)
  7. Download the ZIP file and unzip it on your computer

Inside the archive, you'll find folders with all your photos, plus a JSON file with dates, captions, and locations. The photos are full resolution, often better quality than what Instagram shows in the app.

Upload the photos to a Google Photos album or iCloud folder. That's the link you share with me.

Tips for selecting which posts to include

You don't need every photo. If you've posted 2,000 times over 8 years, that's way too many for one book. Here's how to narrow it down.

  • Pick a time range. One year makes a good book. Five years makes a great one if you're selective. Don't try to fit everything.
  • Skip the filler. Screenshots, memes, promotional posts, and reshares don't belong in a printed book.
  • Keep the photos you'd show someone in person. If you wouldn't pull it up on your phone to show a friend, it probably doesn't need to be in the book.
  • Mix Instagram with phone photos. Your camera roll has behind-the-scenes shots that never made it to Instagram. Those often make the best book pages.

Or skip the selection entirely. Share your whole archive and I'll pick the strongest 50-200 photos depending on the page count you choose. That's part of what's included in the design fee.

Frequently asked questions

Are Instagram photos high enough quality to print?

Yes. Instagram stores photos at 1080x1350 pixels or higher. That's enough for the size they appear in a photo book. The downloaded archive includes the original resolution files, which are often larger than what Instagram displays.

How long does it take to download my Instagram data?

Instagram sends the download link within a few hours to two days, depending on how much data you have. The download itself takes a few minutes. Request it in advance so it's ready when you want to start your book.

Can I include Instagram captions in the book?

Yes. The Instagram archive includes your captions with dates. Send them along and I can use the best ones as page captions. Most people find their Instagram captions are too casual for a printed book, so picking the good ones is worth the time. See tips on photo book captions.

Can I mix Instagram photos with other photos?

Yes. Upload your Instagram archive and your phone photos to the same Google Photos album. I sort everything by date and pick the best shots regardless of source.

What if my account is private or deleted?

You can still download your data from a private account. If the account is already deleted, you have 30 days to request a data download. After that, the photos are gone from Instagram's servers. Another reason to download sooner rather than later.
