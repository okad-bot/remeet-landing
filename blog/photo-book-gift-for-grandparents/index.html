The Best Photo Book Gift for Grandparents

September 2026 · 6 min read

Every holiday season, the same question: what do you get grandparents who don't want more stuff? They have enough candles. They don't need another scarf. What they actually want is to see their grandchildren more.

A custom photo book is the gift that solves this perfectly. Not a generic one — a beautifully designed book that tells the story of the past year, with captions, chapters, and the moments that matter most.

Here's how to make one that they'll keep on the coffee table, not in a drawer.

Why photo books work as gifts

Grandparents aren't on your family group chat seeing every photo in real time. They might get the occasional text with a picture, or see highlights on a visit. A photo book fills in the gaps — all the Tuesday afternoons, random park visits, and bedtime stories they missed.

Unlike a digital album or shared folder, a physical book gets picked up. It sits on a shelf. Visitors see it. It becomes part of the house, not buried in a phone.

What to include in a grandparent book

The essentials

  • Grandparent moments — Any photos of them with the grandchildren. Visits, FaceTime screenshots, holiday gatherings. These are the most meaningful pages in the book.
  • Milestones — First steps, first day of school, lost teeth, birthday parties. Grandparents want to see the big moments even if they weren't there.
  • The everyday — Breakfast messes, backyard play, reading before bed. These are the pages that make grandparents feel connected to daily life.

What makes it special

  • Family resemblance shots — Photos where the grandchild looks just like the parent did at that age. Grandparents notice these immediately.
  • Handwritten-style captions — "She has your laugh, Grandma" or "He asked about you every Tuesday." Personal captions turn a photo book into a love letter.
  • The places — Your kitchen, their backyard, the park between your houses. Places carry memories.
  • Growth comparison — If this is a yearly gift, include a photo from the same spot each year. Kids grow fast; grandparents feel every inch.

Timing: when to order

If you're making this for the holidays, here's the timeline to work backwards from:

  • Remeet: 72-hour proof + 5-7 day shipping = order by mid-December for Christmas
  • DIY services (Mixbook, Shutterfly): Budget 3-5 hours to design + 5-9 day shipping = start by early December
  • Rush options: Most services offer expedited shipping for an extra fee

But don't limit this to Christmas. The best time to give a photo book is when it's unexpected. Grandparents' birthday, Mother's Day, Father's Day, or just because. "Here's this year so far" in July hits harder than a holiday gift.

One book or two?

If both sets of grandparents want a copy, plan for it from the start. Most photo book services offer discounted extra copies:

  • Remeet: Extra grandparent copy for $79 (added to any order)
  • Shutterfly/Mixbook: Reorder the same design at full or near-full price
  • Chatbooks: Duplicate orders at the same per-book rate

If you're making two copies, think about whether each set of grandparents should get the same book or slightly different versions. Some families customize captions — "He talks about Grandma's cookies every week" for one, "She always asks to call Nana" for the other.

The "good enough" approach

The biggest risk with a grandparent photo book isn't that it won't be perfect. It's that it won't get made at all.

You don't need to find every great photo. You don't need perfect captions on every page. You don't need to stress over which layout to use. A book with 60 good photos and simple captions is infinitely better than a perfect book that never gets ordered.

The book that grandparents will treasure isn't the one with the best design — it's the one with their grandchild's face on every other page.

If the thought of designing it yourself is what's stopping you, skip the editor entirely. Share your photos with a service that handles the design. You spend 5 minutes sharing a Google Photos album. They get a book they'll show every visitor for the next year.
