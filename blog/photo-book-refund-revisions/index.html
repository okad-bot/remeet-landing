What If I Don't Like My Photo Book?

Updated Nov 2026 · by Olga, designer at ReMeet, 10 years in editorial design

If you don't love the first draft of your ReMeet photo book, you get a full refund. No questions asked. If you like the direction but want changes, two revision rounds are included in the price. Tell me what to fix in plain words. Your designed book starts at $29.

What happens after you get the first draft

Within 72 hours of sharing your photos, I send you a designed proof as a PDF link. You open it, flip through every page, and decide what you think.

Three things can happen:

  1. You love it. Approve it. Download the PDF or order a printed copy.
  2. You like it but want changes. Send me your revision requests. I update the book within 24 hours. You get two rounds of this.
  3. It's not what you wanted at all. Request a full refund. I process it, no arguments.

Nothing prints until you give the green light. The design fee ($29 for up to 30 pages, $79 for up to 100) covers the draft and both revision rounds. Printing is a separate step, ordered only after you approve.

How to request changes

You don't need to know design terms. Just describe what you want in plain words. Here are real examples of revision requests that work:

  • "Move the beach photo on page 7 to the cover."
  • "Remove the blurry shot on page 12 and replace it with one of the sunset photos."
  • "Add more white space between the photos. It feels too crowded."
  • "Swap pages 4 and 8. I want the birthday before the vacation."
  • "The caption on page 15 should say 'Lake Como, July 2026' not 'Lake Como, June 2026'."
  • "Can you make the fonts bigger? My mom has trouble reading small text."
  • "I'd like a cleaner look overall. Less color, more white."

You can send as many changes as you want in a single round. There's no per-page limit. I'll update everything and send you a new proof within 24 hours.

What "full refund" actually means

If the first draft misses the mark and you don't want to continue, I refund the full design fee. No partial refunds, no credits, no "let's try one more time" pressure. The refund goes back to your original payment method.

This policy exists because I want you to feel safe ordering. Photo books are personal. You're trusting someone with your family photos. If the result doesn't feel right, you shouldn't be stuck with it.

One thing to note: the refund applies to the design fee. If you've already ordered a printed copy and it has shipped, printing and shipping are handled separately. But since nothing prints until you approve, this situation rarely comes up.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a revision?

A revision is any set of changes you request at once. You can ask to swap photos, move pages, adjust captions, add white space, or remove spreads. There is no limit on the number of changes within a single round. You get two rounds included.

Can I change the photo selection after the first draft?

Yes. If you want different photos included, tell me which ones to add or remove. You can reference photos by description or by the date they were taken. I can also swap the cover photo or rearrange the order of pages.

What if I want a completely different style?

If the first draft doesn't match what you had in mind at all, you can request a full refund. If you like the photos but want a different look, describe what you want and I'll redesign it in the next revision round. For example: more white space, bolder fonts, a different color palette.

How long do revisions take?

Each revision round takes about 24 hours. The first draft arrives within 72 hours of sharing your photos. The full process from order to final approval is typically 4 to 6 days.

Do I pay for printing before I approve the book?

No. You pay for the design first ($29 or $79). Printing is a separate step that happens only after you approve the final proof. You can keep the PDF and skip printing entirely, or order a printed copy from $69.
