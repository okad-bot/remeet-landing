How international shipping works

All books are printed in the United States. US and Canada orders ship via USPS or UPS with tracking included. European orders go through an international carrier, also with tracking.

Here is the step-by-step process:

  1. You place your order on remeet.cc and share your photos
  2. I design the book and send you a proof within 72 hours
  3. You review, request changes if needed (two rounds included)
  4. You approve the final version
  5. The book goes to print. Printing takes 3-5 business days
  6. The printed book ships to your address (or to someone else's)
  7. You get a tracking number by email

You can enter any shipping address at checkout. It does not need to be your own. Many people order books shipped directly to grandparents, siblings, or friends in other cities.

Ordering for someone in another country

A photo book makes a great gift for family abroad. Here is how most people do it:

  • One book, one address. Enter the recipient's address at checkout. The book ships directly to them. No need to ship it to yourself first.
  • Same book, multiple addresses. Order extra copies at $69 each. Each copy can go to a different address. Useful for grandparents in different countries - same book, two deliveries.
  • Digital first, print later. If you are not sure about timing, order the $29 designed PDF. Share it digitally right away. Order the printed version when there is no rush.

I have shipped books to parents in Toronto, in-laws in London, and friends in Berlin. The process is the same regardless of where the book is going. You just pick the address.

Frequently asked questions

How long does shipping take to Canada?

7-12 business days after you approve the proof. Shipping to Canada is included in the price. For Christmas delivery, order by December 1.

Do you ship to Australia?

Not yet for printed books. You can order a designed PDF ($29 for 30 pages, $79 for 100 pages) and print it locally at any photo printing service or copy shop.

Can I ship to multiple addresses?

Yes. Order extra copies at $69 each and enter a different shipping address for each one. Useful for sending the same book to grandparents in different cities or countries.

Is tracking included?

Yes. You get a tracking number by email once the book ships. US and Canada shipments use USPS or UPS. European orders use a local carrier with tracking.

Do I pay customs or import tax?

US and Canada: no customs fees. Europe: the +$15 shipping fee covers delivery to your door. Import duties depend on your country, but printed books are exempt from customs duty in most EU countries under cultural goods rules.
