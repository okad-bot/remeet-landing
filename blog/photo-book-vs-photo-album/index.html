Photo Book vs Photo Album: Which One Should You Pick?

Updated Nov 2026
Olga, designer at ReMeet, 10 years in editorial design

A photo book is a bound, printed product with photos and text designed onto the pages. A photo album is a binder with sleeves or sticky pages where you insert individual prints. Photo books are better for gifts and finished keepsakes. Albums are better if you want to add, remove, or rearrange prints over time.

What each one is

A photo book is printed like a magazine or hardcover book. The photos are part of the page, designed with layout, spacing, captions, and backgrounds. You can't remove or swap individual photos after printing. The design is permanent.

A photo album is a binder with slip-in sleeves, peel-and-stick pages, or photo corners. You print individual photos (at home or at a drugstore) and slide them in. You can rearrange, add, or remove photos anytime.

When a photo book makes sense

  • Gifts. A photo book arrives finished. No assembly needed. This matters for grandparents, holiday gifts, and thank-you gifts after events.
  • Travel and events. A trip or wedding has a clear beginning and end. The photos are fixed. A designed book tells that story better than loose prints in sleeves.
  • Multiple copies. Need three copies for three households? A photo book prints from the same file at $69 each. An album means assembling three separate albums by hand.
  • Shelf display. A hardcover or layflat photo book sits on a coffee table like a real book. Most albums look like binders.

When a photo album makes sense

  • Ongoing collections. If you're adding photos over months or years (school photos, sports seasons), an album lets you keep inserting new prints without committing to a design.
  • Kids' artwork and mixed media. Albums with peel-and-stick pages hold drawings, ticket stubs, postcards, and other flat items alongside photos.
  • Handwritten notes. If you want to write by hand next to each photo, an album gives you that space. (Photo books can include scanned handwritten notes too, but it's not the same as writing directly on the page.)
  • Budget. A drugstore album with 4x6 prints is the cheapest physical option. Under $30 total if you print at Walgreens or CVS.

The hybrid approach

Some families do both. They keep a running album at home where they toss in prints throughout the year. At the end of the year, they pick the best photos and have them designed into a photo book for the grandparents or the shelf.

The album is the working draft. The book is the finished version.

Frequently asked questions

Is a photo book better than a photo album?

A photo book is better for a finished, designed product you want to keep or gift. A photo album is better if you want to swap prints in and out over time. Neither is universally better.

How much does a photo book cost compared to an album?

A blank photo album costs $15-$50 plus the cost of printing individual photos ($0.10-$0.30 each). A designed photo book through ReMeet costs $29 for up to 30 pages, with optional printing from $69. DIY photo book services like Shutterfly run $20-$60 for printing.

Do photo albums last longer than photo books?

Both last decades if stored away from direct sunlight and humidity. Photo albums with acid-free pages and photo-safe sleeves protect prints well. Hardcover photo books with lay-flat binding are equally durable. The binding quality matters more than the format.

Can I make a photo book from an old photo album?

Yes. Scan or photograph the prints from your album and share them with me. I'll design a photo book from the scans. This is a good way to preserve old albums that are deteriorating.

Which is better for a gift?

A photo book. It arrives finished and ready to display. A photo album requires the recipient to know you assembled it by hand, which is meaningful but less polished as a gift. For grandparents, a printed photo book is the most popular choice.
