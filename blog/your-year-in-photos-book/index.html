Why one book per year works

You take thousands of photos a year. They sit on your phone, backed up to the cloud, never organized, rarely looked at. By the time your kid is 10, you have 50,000 photos and no idea what happened when.

One book per year changes that. Twelve months, organized by month, with the photos that actually matter. January through December. The holidays, the vacations, the random Tuesday that turned out to be a great day. It all goes in one book.

When you do this every December, something happens. You build a shelf. After five years, you have five matching books that tell the story of your family, year by year. Your kids will flip through them. Your grandparents will keep them by the couch. These are the books that survive.

Making it a yearly tradition

The hardest part is doing the first one. After that, it becomes automatic. Here's how families who've been doing this for years keep it going:

  • Order in December. The year is still fresh. You remember which photos matter. If you wait until March, you'll forget half of it and procrastinate the other half.
  • Keep a shared album running all year. Both parents add their favorites as they go. By December, the album is ready to share.
  • Same format every year. Same page count, same style. The books match on a shelf and the design decision is already made.
  • Order copies for grandparents. Extra copies are $69 each. Many families order two or three so each household gets one. It becomes the holiday gift they actually look forward to.

Frequently asked questions

How many photos do I need for a year-in-photos book?

For a 30-page book, 100 to 200 photos is a good range. For a 100-page book, 300 to 500 gives you room for more detail. You can send more and I'll pick the best ones. See the detailed guide on photo counts.

When is the best time to order?

December or January, when the year is still fresh. Many families order in late December so the book covers January through December. But you can order any time for any year.

Can I include photos from both parents' phones?

Yes. Create a shared Google Photos album and both of you add your photos. I sort everything by date and remove duplicates. The book shows the full year from both perspectives.

What if I have photos from previous years too?

Start with this year. Once you like the result, go back and do previous years one at a time. Many families order their current year first, then fill in the gaps. Your second book gets 10% off the design fee.

Do I need to organize my photos before sharing?

No. Share your entire album for the year. I sort by date, remove duplicates and blurry shots, and pick the best photos for each month. That's part of the design service.
