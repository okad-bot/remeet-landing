How it works

  1. Send me your photos. Share a Google Photos album, iCloud link, or any cloud folder. Don't sort anything. I accept up to 5,000 photos and pick the best ones.
  2. I design your book in 72 hours. Every page is laid out by hand. No templates. Photos are grouped by time, duplicates and blurry shots removed, and the best moments get full-page spreads.
  3. You review and approve. I send you a proof. Want changes? Describe them in plain words ("move the beach photo to page 3", "add more white space"). Two revision rounds included.
  4. Print or keep the PDF. Approve the design, then download your PDF or order a printed hardcover. Printing ships within 5-7 business days. US and Canada shipping included.

Every book is different because every family's year is different. Here's what stays the same: clean layouts, real photos (not stock), and enough white space that each page feels calm, not crammed.

Frequently asked questions

Can a printed photo book arrive by Christmas in Canada?

Yes. Order by December 1, and the printed hardcover ships to Canada with delivery before Christmas. After that date, order a designed PDF by December 20 and print it locally the same day.

Can I send more photos after I place the order?

Yes. Share the extra photos before I start designing (within the first 24 hours). If you want to add photos after the first draft, that counts as a revision round.

Can I order copies for each grandparent?

Yes. Extra printed copies are $69 each. Same book, different shipping addresses. I design the book once, and you order as many copies as you need.

What if I only have phone photos?

Phone photos are exactly what I work with. Share a Google Photos album or iCloud link from your phone. I pick the best shots, remove duplicates and blurry frames, and design the book.

What if I don't like the first draft?

Full refund. Two rounds of changes are included if you want adjustments. Describe what you want in plain words, no design jargon needed.
