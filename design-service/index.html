What's included in the design service

Every Remeet photo book design service package includes the full scope of work — no add-ons, no surprise charges.

Photo curation

We review your entire library and select the strongest photos. Duplicates, blurry shots, and screenshots are filtered out. The best moments are chosen based on quality, variety, and emotional weight.

Chapter structure

Photos are organized into chapters that tell a story — by trip, by season, by milestone. The book has a beginning, middle, and end. It reads like a narrative, not a photo dump.

Captions & text

We add dates, locations, and suggested captions using photo metadata. You can edit them, add personal notes, or keep them minimal. Your choice.

Editorial layout

Every spread is designed individually. Full-bleed hero images, curated grids, white space where it matters. The layout responds to the content — a sunset gets room to breathe, a party gets energy.

Typography & color

Fonts, colors, and spacing are selected to match the mood of your photos. A beach vacation feels different from a winter wedding — the design reflects that.

Cover design

A custom cover with your chosen photo, title, and subtitle. Designed for the printed hardcover — clean, elegant, shelf-worthy.

The process, step by step

  1. Share your photos — Send a Google Photos link, shared album, or Drive folder. No sorting required. We accept thousands of photos at once.
  2. Tell us the context — A few sentences about what matters: "This is our first year as a family of four" or "Focus on the Italy trip." Takes 2 minutes.
  3. We design your book (72 hours) — Our team curates, structures, and designs a complete book. AI handles the heavy lifting; a human designer reviews every page.
  4. Review your proof — You receive a shareable link to flip through the full book. Leave comments on any page.
  5. Revisions (unlimited) — "Swap this photo." "Add a caption here." "Make the cover photo the one from dinner." We revise until you're happy.
  6. Print or share — Order a premium hardcover delivered to your door, or share the digital version with family and friends.

Total timeline: Most books go from photos to finished proof in 3 days. Revisions typically take 1-2 additional rounds. Printing and shipping add 7-10 business days.

Frequently asked questions

How many photos should I send?

As many as you have. Seriously. We've designed books from 200 photos and from 10,000. More photos give us more to work with. Don't pre-sort — that's our job.

Can I choose which photos go in the book?

Absolutely. After we send the first proof, you can swap any photo, request additions, or flag must-include shots. You have full control over the final selection.

What if I don't like the design?

Revisions are unlimited. Most clients request 1-2 rounds of tweaks. If the overall direction isn't right, we'll redesign from a different angle — no extra charge.

How do I share my photos?

Google Photos shared album is the easiest. You can also use Google Drive, Dropbox, or iCloud. We'll walk you through it if you're not sure.

Do you design books for businesses or just personal use?

Both. We've designed annual reports in photo book format, company event books, and portfolio books for photographers. The design service adapts to any context.

Can I order multiple copies?

Yes. Additional printed copies are $99 each. Many clients order 2-3 copies — one for themselves, one for parents, one for the in-laws.
