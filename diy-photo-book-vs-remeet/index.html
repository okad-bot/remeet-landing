The DIY photo book problem

DIY photo book tools promise an easy experience. But here's what actually happens:

  • Choosing photos takes hours — You have 2,000 photos. Scrolling through all of them, picking favorites, second-guessing your choices. Before you know it, an entire evening is gone and you've selected 40.
  • Templates all look the same — Three photos in a grid. Maybe four. The same layout repeated for 60 pages. It ends up looking like a photo dump, not a story.
  • The editor fights you — Drag this photo here. Oops, it cropped your kid's head off. Undo. Try a different layout. The text doesn't fit. The background color clashes. Is this fun yet?
  • You run out of energy — By page 20, you're done. The last 40 pages get whatever photos are left, crammed in without thought. The book you imagined and the book you made are very different things.
  • It sits unfinished — Research shows the average DIY photo book takes 3-4 sessions across multiple weeks to complete. Many never make it to print at all.

The problem isn't motivation. You genuinely want this book to exist. The problem is that designing a beautiful photo book is real work, and most people don't have 10+ hours to spare.
