Sail Beyond Cancer

A nonprofit photo book capturing a season of sailing trips with cancer patients on Lake Champlain, Vermont.

Client
Sail Beyond Cancer Vermont
Contact
Kelli Shonter, Executive Director
Book type
2026 season book
Purpose
Document the sailing season for donors, partners, and other chapters
Designed by
ReMeet

The story

Sail Beyond Cancer takes cancer patients and their families sailing on Lake Champlain. Every summer, volunteers organize dozens of trips, each one documented with photos by crew members and participants. By the end of the season, the organization has hundreds of photos spread across phones and shared albums.

Kelli Shonter, the Executive Director, wanted to turn those photos into a book that could be shared with donors, board members, and other Sail Beyond Cancer chapters across the country. The book needed to capture the spirit of the program: the water, the wind, the people, and the moments of genuine joy that happen when someone forgets about treatment for an afternoon.

The process

Kelli shared a Google Photos album with the season's photos. ReMeet's AI sorted through the collection, identified the strongest images, removed duplicates and blurry shots, and organized everything chronologically. A designer then built the book page by page: full-bleed sailing shots, candid moments on deck, group portraits at the dock. The layout varied from spread to spread to match the energy of each trip.

The first proof was delivered within 72 hours. After a round of feedback, the final book was approved and ready for printing.

"I LOVE IT. So excited for this."
Kelli Shonter, Executive Director, Sail Beyond Cancer Vermont
"I plan to share the final product with the other Sail Beyond Cancer chapters. I'd like to do this annually."
Kelli Shonter

Why it matters

For nonprofits, a printed photo book is more than a keepsake. It's a fundraising tool, a board report, and a way to show new volunteers what the program actually looks like. Kelli's plan to make this an annual tradition means every season gets preserved, and every chapter gets to see what the Vermont team accomplished.

ReMeet made it possible without asking Kelli to spend hours in a photo book editor. She shared the photos, reviewed the proof, and had a finished book ready to share with her team.
