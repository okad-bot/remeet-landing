Sail Beyond Cancer: a 2026 season book

How ReMeet turned hundreds of photos from a cancer survivor's Caribbean sailing journey into a 100-page keepsake.

Kelli, a cancer survivor, had hundreds of photos from sailing the Caribbean with Sail Beyond Cancer. She tried making the book herself twice and stopped at page eight. ReMeet designed a 100-page hardcover in 72 hours, organized by island.

The project

Kelli is a cancer survivor. After treatment, she sailed the Caribbean with Sail Beyond Cancer, a nonprofit that takes survivors on healing voyages. Months at sea, dozens of islands, hundreds of photos. She came to ReMeet with a simple request: turn it all into a book that captures the spirit of the trip, not just the scenery.

What made this project special was the emotional weight behind every image. These were not vacation snapshots. They were proof of survival, of freedom, of a life reclaimed.

The challenge

Kelli had hundreds of photos from months of travel. Many were similar: ocean horizons, boat decks, sunsets that all looked beautiful but blurred together. Picking which moments to keep and which to leave out felt impossible, especially when each one carried a memory.

She had tried making the book herself twice. Both times she stopped at page eight.

What ReMeet did

ReMeet's AI sorted every photo, flagged duplicates, and grouped shots by location and time. The designer took it from there: organizing the book into chronological chapters that followed the journey island by island.

Full-bleed ocean spreads gave the book room to breathe. Portraits and group shots anchored each chapter. Handwritten-style captions added a personal, journal-like feel that matched the spirit of the voyage.

"I wanted a book that felt like the journey, not just photos in a grid. When I opened the proof, I cried. Every page brought me back to a moment I was afraid I would forget." Kelli, Sail Beyond Cancer
"I tried making this book myself twice. I got to page 8 both times and gave up. Too many photos, too many memories competing for space. ReMeet figured it out in three days." Kelli

The result

  • 100-page hardcover photo book
  • Delivered in 72 hours from first upload
  • One round of revisions
  • Chronological chapter structure with full-bleed spreads
