Why dads love photo books

Dads don't ask for things. Ask your dad what he wants for Father's Day and he'll say "nothing" or "I don't need anything." That's exactly why a photo book works. He'd never buy it for himself, never think to make one, and never expect it. But hand him a book filled with photos of his kids growing up, family trips, and everyday moments — and he won't put it down.

It says what's hard to say. A photo book doesn't need a long speech. The photos say "I remember this" and "these moments mattered." For dads who aren't big on words, a book of shared memories communicates more than a card ever could.

It gets better with time. Unlike most gifts, a photo book becomes more valuable every year. The kids get older. The memories get further away. And that book on the shelf becomes the place the whole family goes to remember what it was like when everyone was younger.

Gift ideas by dad type

New dad

His first year of fatherhood — the hospital, the sleepless nights, the first smile, the first steps. He was so busy being there that he didn't realize how many incredible moments were piling up. Show him.

Grandpa

A book of grandkid photos is the gift every grandpa wants but would never ask for. Include visits, holidays, video call screenshots, school photos — the whole relationship in one place.

Adventure dad

Hiking, camping, road trips, coaching. A book organized around the adventures you've had together. Chapter titles like "The time we got lost" and "Saturday morning games" write themselves.

Coach dad

Seasons of sideline photos, team celebrations, practice pickups, and post-game dinners. For the dad who spent every weekend at the field, a book that says "we noticed everything you did."

Frequently asked questions

When should I order for Father's Day delivery?

Order at least two weeks before Father's Day for a printed book. We design in 72 hours, then printing and shipping take 7-10 business days. Order by late May to be safe. If you're cutting it close, a digital book is ready instantly and can be shared on Father's Day morning.

What photos should I include?

Send everything — we'll curate the best ones. Great photos for a dad book include: him with the kids at any age, family trips, holidays, coaching moments, backyard hangouts, and the candid shots where he's just being Dad. Don't overthink it. The everyday moments often make the best pages.

Can the kids help choose photos?

Yes, and they should. Kids pick different photos than adults — they remember different moments. Let them scroll through and flag their favorites. We can also include their handwritten notes or drawings as pages in the book if you scan or photograph them.

Can you ship directly to Dad?

Yes. You can ship the book directly to Dad's address with a gift message. Or ship it to yourself first if you want to wrap it and give it in person. Either way, free US shipping is included.

Can I include a dedication page?

Absolutely. Most Father's Day books include a dedication — something from the kids, a note from you, or a family message. Just send us the text and we'll design a beautiful opening page. It's included at no extra cost.
