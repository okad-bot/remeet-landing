Questions

Is the preview really free?

Yes. You see your first spread in 6 styles right away, and you only pay if you want the whole book.

Why only 6 photos?

That's enough to fill one spread and see how your photos look in each style. After you order, you add the rest.

What if I have 500 photos?

Send them all after you order. With the designer picks add-on I choose the best shots for you.

How long does the whole book take?

First full draft by email in 72 hours after you add your photos. Printing takes 3 to 5 working days, plus shipping.

Can I change things?

Yes. You see the whole book before it prints, and two rounds of edits are included.

I only want the PDF. Can I print later?

Yes. Order the PDF now and add a hardcover anytime for $60.

Can I order copies for family?

Yes. Each extra copy of the same book is $69, shipping included in the US. I'll offer it after you order.

Do you ship outside the US?

US and Canada for now. Write to me for other countries.

What happens to my photos?

They're used only for your book and deleted after 60 days if you don't order.
