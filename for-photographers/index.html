Photographer Partnership

Turn your shoots into keepsakes your clients will treasure

Your clients already have the photos. ReMeet turns them into professionally designed photo books - real design, not templates. You get a new revenue stream with zero extra work.

Partner with ReMeet to offer photo books to your photography clients. Share a gallery link, we design the book, your client reviews and approves. Your studio gets title page credit. First book free. No minimums.

How it works

1

Share the gallery

After a shoot, send us your client's gallery link - from any platform you already use. That's all we need to get started.

2

We design the book

Our designers curate the best shots and lay out a book with real typography and thoughtful pacing. No templates, no auto-fill.

3

Client reviews

Your client gets a digital proof to review. Two rounds of revisions are included. They approve when it's right.

4

Deliver the book

We print on premium paper with lay-flat binding and ship directly to your client. Your studio gets credit on the title page.

Why partner with ReMeet

  • New revenue per booking. Add $100-200 to every session by offering a photo book as an upsell. No extra shooting, editing, or layout work on your end.
  • Real design, not templates. Every book is laid out by a designer who curates your shots, sets the pacing, and handles typography. Your work looks its best.
  • A better client experience. Your clients get a tangible keepsake from their session - something they keep on the shelf and show to family.
  • Your studio credit on the title page. Every book includes your studio name and logo, reinforcing your brand every time the book is opened.
  • No inventory, no hassle. You don't stock, ship, or handle customer support. We manage the entire production and fulfillment process.

Questions

Can my clients order directly?

Yes. Clients can go to remeet.cc and order on their own using your PHOTO-* promo code for 15% off. You can also submit a gallery link on their behalf if they prefer a hands-off experience.

Do you print and ship?

We handle everything - design, printing, and shipping. Books are printed on premium paper with lay-flat binding and shipped directly to your client's door.

Can I see a sample?

Absolutely. Send us photos from a recent shoot and we'll create a free PDF proof. No payment, no commitment. That's what the free first book perk is for.

What gallery platforms work?

Any platform where we can access the photos. Pic-Time, ShootProof, Pixieset, Google Photos, Dropbox, iCloud - if you can share a link, we can work with it.
