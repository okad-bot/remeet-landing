How to Make a Photo Book in 2026

September 2026 · 8 min read

You have the photos. You want a book. But there are dozens of services, three fundamentally different approaches, and a lot of "it depends." Here's a clear guide to your options.

The three approaches

Approach 1: Design it yourself (DIY)

This is the traditional route. You choose a service, pick photos, and design every page.

Best services for DIY:

  • Artifact Uprising — Premium quality, beautiful materials. Templates are clean and modern. Expect 4-6 hours for a 60-page book.
  • Mixbook — More affordable, good template variety, intuitive editor. Their AI "Story Mode" can auto-arrange photos to save time.
  • Shutterfly — Budget-friendly, especially with frequent 40-50% sales. Editor is functional but feels dated.

Step by step:

  1. Choose your photos (the hardest part — budget 1-2 hours for curation)
  2. Upload to the service
  3. Choose a template or start blank
  4. Place photos, adjust layouts, add text
  5. Review every page
  6. Order

Best for: People who enjoy the creative process and have a free afternoon.

Approach 2: Auto-generate

Several services can auto-create a book from your photo library with minimal input.

Best services for auto-generation:

  • Chatbooks — Connects to Instagram or camera roll. Auto-generates monthly books. Very affordable ($10-15 softcover).
  • Google Photos — Built-in "Print store" feature creates basic books from albums. Starting at ~$15.
  • Apple Photos — macOS can auto-generate a book from your library. Decent layout suggestions.

Step by step:

  1. Connect your photo source
  2. Select an album or date range
  3. Let the service auto-arrange
  4. Optionally rearrange or remove photos
  5. Order

Best for: Quick, affordable books. Monthly subscriptions. People who don't need editorial design.

The trade-off: Minimal curation (every photo goes in), basic layouts, no chapters or captions.

Approach 3: Done-for-you

You share your photos and someone else designs the book. You review a proof and request changes.

Services that offer this:

  • Remeet — Share any number of photos, get a complete editorial book in 72 hours. Full curation, chapters, captions. $69 digital, $199 printed.
  • Artifact Uprising Design Service — $29-199 depending on the service tier. They'll design your album for you.

Step by step:

  1. Share your photos (Google Photos link, Drive folder, or upload)
  2. Add any notes about what matters most
  3. Wait for your proof (24-72 hours)
  4. Request changes in plain language
  5. Approve and order

Best for: People with large photo libraries who want a beautiful result without spending hours on design. Parents, busy professionals, anyone who's been "meaning to do it" for months.

Quick decision guide

I have a free afternoon and enjoy designing: DIY with Mixbook or Artifact Uprising

I want something cheap and fast: Auto-generate with Chatbooks or Google Photos

I want it beautiful and don't want to do the work: Done-for-you with Remeet

I've been meaning to make one for 6+ months: Done-for-you — the best book is the one that gets made

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Including too many photos. A 60-page book with 80 photos has room to breathe. The same book crammed with 300 photos feels like a contact sheet.
  • Skipping captions. Even short ones add enormous value: "The morning everything changed" or "Her favorite corner."
  • Waiting for the perfect time. There's never a perfect weekend. Start with what you have.
  • Spending weeks in the editor. Set a time limit. Done is better than perfect.
