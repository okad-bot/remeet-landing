Good questions

You've got questions. We've got answers.

Still curious?
We're here ♥

How many photos should I send?

Anything from roughly 50 to 5,000. We curate the best moments for your story.

Do I need to choose my favorite photos?

No. Send us everything — we'll find the best ones. That's what we do.

What if you leave out an important photo?

Just tell us. Revisions are unlimited before printing. Say "add the one at the beach" and we will.

What if I don't like the design?

You get a full proof to review. Request as many changes as you need — in plain language, not an editor.

Do I need to use an editor?

No. There's no editor, no templates, no dragging and dropping. You just talk to us.

Are my photos private?

Yes. Your photos are encrypted in transit and at rest. We delete them 90 days after your book is complete.

Can I order copies for grandparents?

Yes. Additional copies start at $179 each, with volume discounts — and can be shipped directly to family.

When will I receive my book?

First proof in 72 hours. After you approve, printing takes 5-7 business days plus shipping.
