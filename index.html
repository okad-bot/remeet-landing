Good questions

You've got questions. We've got answers.

Can I send an iCloud or Google Photos link?

Yes, that's the easiest way. On iPhone, select your photos, tap Share, then Copy iCloud Link. Please don't use a Shared Album: Apple shrinks photos there, and they print soft. Google Photos album links work as they are.

Do you use AI?

Only to sort and pick photos. We never change or generate your photos. Every page is designed and checked by a person.

How many photos should I send?

Anything from roughly 50 to 5,000. We curate the best moments for your story.

Do I need to choose my favorite photos?

No. Send us everything, we'll find the best ones. That's what we do.

What if you leave out an important photo?

Just tell us. Say "add the one at the beach" and we will. Two rounds of edits are included, and we take any changes you send within a week of the first proof.

What if I don't like the design?

You get a full proof to review. Ask for changes in plain words, no editor needed. Two rounds of edits are included within a week of the first proof.

Do I need to use an editor?

No. There's no editor, no templates, no dragging and dropping. You just talk to us.

Are my photos private?

Yes. Your photos are encrypted in transit and at rest. We delete them 90 days after your book is complete.

Can I get a printed book?

Yes. You pay for printing after you approve the design. Classic hardcover: $25 up to 30 pages, $49 up to 100, plus US shipping $7.60. Premium layflat: $89 or $149, US shipping included. For Canada and Europe, I'll include shipping in your quote.

When will I receive my book?

Your first proof comes in 72 hours. You have a week for edits. After you approve, printing takes 3–5 working days, plus delivery. For Christmas, order and send your photos by December 5 (US and Canada).
