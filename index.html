Questions and answers +
How does it work?

Pick a book and pay, $29 or $79. Then upload your photos or paste an album link. I pick the best shots, design every page and send the first draft to your email in 72 hours. Want to see it first? Try a free preview with 6 photos.

Can I send an iCloud or Google Photos link?

Yes. After you order, upload your photos on the site or paste an iCloud or Google Photos link. On iPhone, select your photos, tap Share, then Copy iCloud Link. Please don't use a Shared Album: Apple shrinks photos there, and they print soft.

How many photos should I send?

Anything from about 50 to 5,000. Upload up to 300 on the site or paste an album link for more. I pick the best ones for you, that's included.

Do you use AI?

Only to sort photos. I never change or generate your photos. Every page is designed and checked by a person.

How much does it cost?

A small book up to 30 pages is $29 and a big book up to 100 pages is $79, both as a PDF, with photo picking and 2 rounds of edits included. Printing is extra, once you've approved your book.

How much is printing?

Once you've approved your book: hardcover +$60, premium layflat +$110, US shipping included. Extra copies are $69 each.

What if I don't like the design?

Ask for changes in plain words, 2 rounds of edits are included. Don't love the first draft? Full refund.

Do I need to use an editor?

No. No editor, no templates, no dragging and dropping. You just reply to my email.

Are my photos private?

Yes. Your photos are encrypted in transit and at rest, used only for your book, and deleted 90 days after your book is complete.

When will I get my book?

The first draft comes in 72 hours after your photos are in. You have a week for edits, or 2 to 3 days for Christmas orders. After you approve, printing takes 3 to 5 working days, plus shipping. Order and send your photos by Dec 1 for Christmas delivery.

Do you ship outside the US?

US only for now.
