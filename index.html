Questions and answers +
How does it work?

Order, send me an album link or a zip, get your first draft in 48 hours or less, ask for changes, print if you want. Want to see it first? Try a free preview with 6 photos.

Can I send an iCloud or Google Photos link?

Yes, that's the easiest way. After you order, paste an iCloud, Google Photos or Google Drive link, or email me a zip. On iPhone, select your photos, tap Share, then Copy iCloud Link. Please don't use a Shared Album: Apple shrinks photos there, and they print soft.

How many photos should I send?

The photos you want in the book, up to 300, as one album link or a zip. No time to pick? Send me up to 3,000 and I'll choose the best and group them for you, +$25 at checkout.

Do you use AI?

Only to sort photos. I never change or generate your photos. Every page is designed and checked by a person.

How much does it cost?

A small book up to 30 pages is $29 and a big book up to 100 pages is $79, both as a PDF, with photo picking and 2 rounds of edits included. Printing is extra, once you've approved your book.

How much is printing?

Once you've approved your book. Hardcover: $69 for a small book, $119 for a big one. Premium layflat: $129 or $199. Copies for family $69 each. US and Canada shipping included, Europe +$15.

What if I don't like the design?

Ask for changes in plain words, 2 rounds of edits are included. Don't love the first draft? Full refund.

Do I need to use an editor?

No. No editor, no templates, no dragging and dropping. You just reply to my email.

Are my photos private?

Yes. Your photos are encrypted in transit and at rest, used only for your book, and deleted 90 days after your book is complete.

When will I get my book?

The first draft comes in 48 hours or less after your photos are in. You have a week for edits, or 2 to 3 days for Christmas orders. After you approve, printing takes 3 to 5 working days, plus shipping. Order and send your photos by Dec 1 for Christmas delivery.

Do you ship outside the US?

Yes. Shipping to the US and Canada is included, Europe is +$15. Worldwide shipping also available, just ask.
