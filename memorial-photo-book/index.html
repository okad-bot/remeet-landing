Why a memorial photo book matters

After losing someone, photos become the most precious thing you have. But they're spread across decades, devices, and family members. Some are in frames, some are in albums nobody opens, and most are buried in a camera roll alongside grocery lists and screenshots.

A memorial book gathers all of it — the childhood photos your aunt has, the vacation snapshots your cousin took, the selfie from last Thanksgiving — and organizes it into the story of a life. It becomes something the family passes around, something grandchildren open on quiet evenings, something that keeps a person present even after they're gone.

We handle the design with the care this project deserves. You focus on gathering the photos and the stories. We'll take it from there.

What goes inside a memorial book

A life in chapters

We organize photos into meaningful chapters — childhood, family life, career, travels, the people they loved. A structure that tells who they were, not just what they looked like.

Their words and stories

Include their favorite sayings, excerpts from letters, or stories that family members want preserved. We'll design these alongside the photos with the care they deserve.

Photos from every era

Black-and-white childhood portraits alongside last year's birthday dinner. We blend different eras and photo qualities into a cohesive, beautiful layout.

A keepsake for the family

Order copies for every household. A book that sits on the shelf, gets pulled out at family gatherings, and helps the next generation know who they came from.

How it works

  1. Gather photos from family — Create a shared Google Photos album or Drive folder and invite family members to add their photos. Old prints? Scan them with your phone. We work with whatever you have.
  2. Share any stories or context — Tell us about the person: their passions, the places that mattered, the people they loved. Include quotes, letters, or anything you'd like in the book.
  3. We design the book with care — Our team curates the photos, organizes them into a meaningful story, adds captions, and creates a layout that honors who they were. Ready in 72 hours.
  4. Review at your own pace — There's no rush. Look through the proof, share it with family, and request any changes you'd like. Take as much time as you need.
  5. Print and share — Premium hardcover, archival-quality paper. Order copies for every family member. Each can be shipped to a different address.

Tips for gathering photos

  • Ask the whole family — Every person has different photos. Aunts, cousins, childhood friends. The more perspectives, the richer the book.
  • Include everyday moments — Not just formal portraits. The candid shots, the blurry holiday ones, the ones that capture how they really were.
  • Scan old prints — Use your phone camera or a free scanning app. Even imperfect scans carry powerful memories.
  • Add context — "This is at Aunt Helen's house, 1987" or "He told this joke every Thanksgiving." Details make the book personal.
  • Don't worry about quality — We work with whatever you have. A slightly blurry photo of a meaningful moment is worth more than a perfect photo of nothing.

Frequently asked questions

Can multiple family members contribute photos?

Yes. Share a Google Photos album or Drive folder and invite family members to add their photos. We'll curate from everything that's shared, bringing together memories from different people and different eras.

How do you handle sensitive or emotional content?

With great care. Our designers understand the weight of this project. We approach every memorial book with sensitivity, thoughtful pacing, and respect for the person being honored. You have full control over every page before printing.

Can I include scanned old photos or printed photographs?

Absolutely. Scan old photos with your phone camera or a scanner and include them alongside digital photos. We work with whatever you have — the mix of old and new often makes the most beautiful books.

Can I add their own words, quotes, or stories?

Yes. Share any text you'd like included — their favorite sayings, excerpts from letters, stories family members want to tell. We'll weave them into the design alongside the photos.

How long does the process take?

Your first proof is ready within 72 hours. From there, take as much time as you need with revisions — there's no rush. When you're ready, printing and shipping takes 7-10 business days.

Can I order copies for extended family?

Yes. Many families order multiple copies — one for each household. Additional copies start at $179, with discounts for larger orders. Each copy can be shipped to a different address.
