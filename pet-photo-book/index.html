Why make a pet photo book

Photos on a phone don't tell a story. A book does. We'll organize your photos into chapters that show who your pet really is — the goofy side, the gentle side, the side that steals food off the counter.

Chapter ideas built in

We create natural chapters from your photos: "The puppy days," "Adventures," "Best friends," "Nap champion." You don't have to think of the structure — we find it in your photos.

A lasting memorial

Pets don't stay forever. A photo book becomes the place you go to remember — not scrolling through a phone, but holding a real book with their whole story in your hands.

The perfect gift

For the dog mom in your life, the cat dad, or anyone who treats their pet like family. A custom pet photo book is personal, unexpected, and impossible to forget.

Frequently asked questions

Can I include multiple pets in one book?

Absolutely. Many families have more than one pet, and the book is better for it. We can organize chapters by pet, by timeline, or weave them together — however your life actually looks.

What if most of my photos look the same?

That's completely normal — pets have a favorite sleeping spot and you have a favorite angle. Our designers are skilled at finding the subtle differences that make each photo worth keeping. We'll pick the strongest variations and create layouts that feel dynamic, not repetitive.

Do you write captions for pets?

Yes. We write captions that capture your pet's personality based on what you tell us. Things like "The couch is his" or "She barks at every delivery driver like it's a personal offense." Send us a few notes about their quirks and we'll take it from there.

Can this be a memorial book for a pet who passed away?

Yes, and we handle these books with care. A memorial pet photo book is one of the most meaningful things you can create — a permanent record of the life you shared. We can include a dedication page, organize photos chronologically, and help you tell their whole story from beginning to end.

How many photos should I send?

Anywhere from 50 to 2,000+. More is better — we'll curate the best shots and find moments you might have overlooked. Don't worry about sorting or picking favorites. That's our job.

Can I add text and stories about my pet?

Yes. You can send us stories, funny moments, personality traits, or anything you want included. We'll weave your words into the book alongside the photos — as captions, as pull quotes, or as dedicated story pages between photo spreads.
