What goes in a pregnancy photo book

  • The positive test — That first photo, the reaction, telling your partner or family
  • Bump progression — Weekly or monthly bump photos showing the transformation
  • Ultrasound scans — First heartbeat, gender reveal, 3D scans — all printed beautifully
  • Nursery preparation — Painting the room, assembling the crib, the tiny clothes laid out and waiting
  • Cravings and quirks — The midnight snack runs, the food aversions, the things only pregnancy makes you do
  • Baby shower — The people who showed up to celebrate, the gifts, the games, the cake
  • Letters to baby — Notes you wrote during pregnancy, hopes, dreams, things you want them to know
  • Delivery day — The hospital bag, the drive, the first moment you held your baby

Chapters by trimester

First trimester: the secret

The positive test, the disbelief, the excitement you could barely contain. Morning sickness, first doctor visits, and the tiny secret you carried everywhere. These early weeks feel surreal — your book captures the quiet beginning.

Second trimester: the glow

The bump appears. You start feeling kicks. The nursery plans take shape. Gender reveal, baby shower, registry shopping. This is when pregnancy starts to feel real, and the photos start piling up.

Third trimester: the countdown

Hospital bag packed. Nursery finished. Every day feels like it could be the day. The anticipation, the last photos as a family of two (or three, or four), and finally — the moment everything changes.

Include your ultrasound scans

Ultrasound photos are some of the most meaningful images from your pregnancy, but they fade on thermal paper and get lost in phone galleries. We'll scan and print them at high quality, integrated into your book's design alongside your everyday photos.

Whether it's the first grainy heartbeat image or a detailed 3D profile, your ultrasounds become a permanent part of the story.

Why a maternity photo book matters

Pregnancy is temporary. The book is forever. Nine months feels like a long time when you're living it, but afterward? It blurs. The bump photos sit in your camera roll, mixed with screenshots and grocery lists. A photo book gives those months the weight they deserve.

It's the first chapter of your child's story. Someday your child will want to know: What did you look like when you were carrying me? What was the nursery like before I arrived? What did you eat, crave, dream about? This book answers all of that.
