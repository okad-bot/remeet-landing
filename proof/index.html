Questions

Is the spread really free?

Yes. You only pay if you love it and want the whole book.

Why up to 30 photos if a spread uses only a few?

Picking is half the job. A spread holds 2 to 6 photos, and seeing which ones I choose shows you how I'll build the whole book.

What if I have 500 photos?

Send your favorite 10 to 30 now. After you pick a style, I'll ask for the rest by email.

How long does the whole book take?

First full draft in 72 hours after payment. Printing takes 3 to 5 working days, plus shipping.

Can I change things?

Yes. You see the full draft before it prints.

Can I get only the digital file?

Yes, choose "Design only (PDF)" at checkout.

Do you ship outside the US?

Currently US and Canada. Write to me for other countries.

What happens to my photos?

They're used only for your book and deleted after 60 days if you don't order.
