Quality & Materials

Materials matter

Your photos deserve more than thin paper and a glued spine. Every Remeet book is made with archival-quality materials chosen to look and feel exceptional — and to last for generations.

Cover

The cover is the first thing you touch, so it needs to feel substantial. Remeet books use a rigid hardcover board wrapped in natural linen cloth. The texture is warm and tactile — closer to a journal you'd find in a design shop than a standard photo book.

Your book's title is debossed (pressed into the linen) rather than printed on top. This creates a subtle, elegant impression that won't fade, peel, or scratch over time. The debossing is done with a custom die for each book, so the typography matches the editorial style of the interior.

The spine is reinforced for repeated use. Pull this book off the shelf a thousand times — it will hold its shape.

Paper

Inside, every page is printed on 200gsm archival-quality stock with a satin finish. Here's what that means in plain terms:

  • 200gsm weight — About twice as thick as a magazine page. Feels sturdy and luxurious. No bleed-through from the other side.
  • Archival quality — Acid-free and lignin-free paper that resists yellowing. The same standard used by museums and fine-art printers for works that need to last decades.
  • Satin finish — A soft, low-glare surface that makes colors rich without the fingerprint-magnet shine of glossy paper. Photos look natural — the way they looked on your screen when you first loved them.

Binding

Remeet books use lay-flat binding (also called flush-mount or seamless binding). Unlike standard photo books where pages curve into the spine and the center of each spread disappears, lay-flat pages open completely flat.

This matters because the best photos often span two pages. A panoramic landscape, a group shot, a wide scene from your wedding — with lay-flat binding, nothing gets lost in the gutter. Every spread looks like a single, uninterrupted image.

The binding is stitched, not just glued. This makes it more durable and allows the book to open and close smoothly for years without pages loosening.

Printing

Color accuracy matters. A sunset should look like the sunset you remember, not an oversaturated version of it.

We use professional-grade digital printing with full ICC color management. Every print run is calibrated against reference standards so colors are accurate and consistent from the first page to the last. Skin tones look natural. Shadows have detail. Whites are clean.

Resolution is printed at 300 DPI — the same standard used by professional photography labs. Even photos taken on an iPhone look sharp and detailed at full-page size.

That's the whole point.
