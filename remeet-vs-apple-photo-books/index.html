Remeet vs Apple Photo Books

Apple discontinued its built-in photo book service and now recommends third-party partners. Here's how Remeet compares to those options — and why it might be the upgrade you've been looking for.

What happened to Apple Photo Books?

Apple shut down its native photo printing service in 2018. If you used to make photo books directly from the Photos app on your Mac, that feature no longer exists. Apple now recommends third-party extensions like Motif and Mimeo Photos, which integrate with Apple Photos but are separate products with their own pricing and quality.

Choose Apple Partners (Motif / Mimeo) if:

  • You want to work directly inside Apple Photos on your Mac
  • You're looking for the closest experience to the original Apple Books
  • You enjoy designing layouts yourself and want hands-on control
  • You want a solid book at a moderate price point
  • All your photos are in Apple Photos and you don't want to export them

Choose Remeet if:

  • You miss the simplicity of Apple Books and want something even easier
  • You want professional editorial design without learning another editor
  • Your photos are scattered across iCloud, Google Photos, and your phone
  • You want a premium book with chapters, captions, and storytelling
  • You've been putting off making a photo book since Apple shut theirs down

The honest take

Apple's photo book service was beloved because it was seamless — it lived right inside Photos, the design was clean, and the quality was reliable. When Apple discontinued it, a lot of people simply stopped making photo books. The third-party replacements (Motif, Mimeo) are decent, but they've never quite matched the frictionless experience Apple offered.

Remeet doesn't try to replicate the Apple Books experience. Instead, it removes even more friction: you don't design anything at all. Share your photos from any source, and we create a professionally designed book with editorial curation, chapters, and captions. You review, request changes in plain language, and approve.

If you want the closest thing to old Apple Books, the partner extensions are your best bet. If what you actually loved about Apple Books was how easy it was — and you wish it were even easier — Remeet is worth considering.
