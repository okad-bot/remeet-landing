Choose Artifact Uprising if:

  • You enjoy the design process and want full creative control
  • You have a specific vision for your book's layout
  • You want to choose from multiple book formats (layflat, softcover, board book)
  • You prefer to select every photo yourself

Choose Remeet if:

  • You have hundreds or thousands of photos and don't want to sort them
  • You'd rather review a proof than design from scratch
  • You want editorial-quality design without learning an editor
  • You've been meaning to make a photo book but haven't found the time
  • You need extra copies for grandparents at a reasonable price

The honest take

Artifact Uprising makes excellent products. Their materials are premium, their templates are clean, and if you enjoy designing, their editor is one of the best. They're the gold standard for DIY photo books.

Remeet exists for people who want that level of quality but don't want to do the design work. You get professional editorial design — chapters, curation, captions — without opening an editor. The trade-off is less control over individual layout decisions and a higher per-book price.

Both produce books you'll be proud to display. The question is whether you'd rather spend 5 hours designing or 5 minutes sharing.
