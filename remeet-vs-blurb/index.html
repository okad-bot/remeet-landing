Remeet vs Blurb

Blurb gives you professional-grade design tools. Remeet designs the book for you. Two very different approaches to the same goal.

RemeetBlurb

Your time5 minutes (upload photos)10-40+ hours (design the entire book)
Photo curationAI selects best photos from your libraryYou select and place every photo manually
Design toolNo tool needed — we handle itBookWright (desktop app), PDF upload, or InDesign plugin
Design qualityEditorial — magazine-style, AI + human reviewDepends entirely on your design skills

Turnaround72-hour proofInstant (you design it, then order prints)

Best forLarge photo libraries, hands-off designPhotographers, designers, self-publishers

Choose Remeet if:

  • You have hundreds or thousands of photos and no time to sort them
  • You want a professionally designed book without learning layout software
  • You'd rather upload your photos and get a finished result than spend weekends designing
  • You don't have experience with graphic design tools
  • You want AI to find your best photos across a messy camera roll

Choose Blurb if:

  • You're a photographer or designer who wants pixel-perfect control
  • You already have a layout ready in InDesign, Lightroom, or similar
  • You're self-publishing a portfolio, cookbook, or art book
  • You want the widest range of print formats and paper types
  • You enjoy the design process as much as the finished product

The middle ground

These two services barely overlap. Blurb is a professional publishing tool that happens to print photo books. Remeet is a photo book service that happens to use AI. If you know your way around BookWright or InDesign and want total control, Blurb is unmatched. If you want a beautiful book from your camera roll and the idea of opening a desktop layout app makes you tired, Remeet handles the entire process for you.

One isn't better than the other — they solve different problems for different people.

Questions people ask

Is Blurb or Remeet better for a family photo book?

For most families, Remeet is the easier choice. You upload your photos and get a designed book back in 72 hours without learning any software. Blurb is better if you have design skills and want full creative control over every spread.

Can I upload a PDF to Remeet like I can with Blurb?

No. Remeet works differently — you upload your photos and the AI curates and designs the book for you. PDF upload is a Blurb feature aimed at designers who create layouts in InDesign or similar tools.

Which is cheaper, Remeet or Blurb?

Blurb's printing starts lower (around $20 for a small softcover), but you do all the design work yourself. Remeet starts at $29 for a 30-page digital book with AI curation and human designer review included. If you value your time, Remeet's price includes the design service that Blurb does not offer.
