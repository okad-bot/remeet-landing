Choose Chatbooks if:

  • You want a quick, affordable book every month
  • You don't need editorial design — just photos in print
  • Budget is the top priority
  • You want a casual record, not a keepsake

Choose Remeet if:

  • You want a book that looks like it was made by a designer
  • You have thousands of photos and need someone to curate the best ones
  • You want chapters, captions, and a story — not just photos in order
  • It's for a special occasion (first year, big trip, gift for grandparents)
  • You want something coffee-table worthy

The honest take

Chatbooks is great at what it does: fast, cheap, automatic photo books from your camera roll or Instagram. It's the best option for casual monthly documentation — a running record of your family's life at $10-15/book.

Remeet is a different product entirely. It's for the moments that deserve more than a basic auto-layout: your baby's first year, the big family trip, a gift that grandparents will keep on the shelf. You pay more, but you get editorial design, curation, and a book that reads like a story.

Many families use both: Chatbooks for the monthly record, Remeet for the annual keepsake.
