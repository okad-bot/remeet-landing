Choose Mixbook if:

  • You enjoy designing and want creative control over every page
  • You want to try their AI Story Mode for a semi-automated experience
  • Budget matters — Mixbook runs frequent sales (40-50% off)
  • You want other products too (cards, calendars, wall art)
  • You have a clear vision and want to execute it yourself

Choose Remeet if:

  • You don't want to learn a photo book editor
  • You have thousands of photos and need curation
  • You want professional editorial design, not templates
  • You've started a Mixbook project and never finished it
  • You want to tell someone "add more beach photos" instead of dragging and dropping

The honest take

Mixbook is probably the best DIY photo book editor available today. It's intuitive, their templates are well-designed, and Story Mode is a genuine innovation — it can auto-arrange your photos into a decent layout. If you enjoy the creative process, it's the right choice.

The reality is that many Mixbook projects never get finished. The editor is great, but "great editor" still means hours of work. Remeet exists for the people who want the end result — the beautiful book — without the project.

You pay more with Remeet (especially vs Mixbook's sale prices), but you're paying for professional design time, not just printing. If that trade-off makes sense for your time and budget, it's worth it.
