Choose Motif if:

  • You use a Mac and organize your photos in Apple Photos
  • You like the idea of building a book without leaving your photo library
  • You want a physical printed book shipped to your door
  • You enjoy choosing layouts and placing photos yourself
  • You want a simple book from a small, pre-selected set of photos

Choose Remeet if:

  • You have hundreds or thousands of unsorted photos and want someone to handle it
  • You're not on a Mac, or your photos aren't only in Apple Photos
  • You want professional editorial design, not template-based layout
  • You don't have time to sit in a photo book editor for hours
  • You want AI to deeply curate your library — not just suggest, but decide

The honest take

Motif's biggest strength is convenience for Apple users. If your photos already live in Apple Photos, opening Motif feels seamless — you browse your library, pick an album, and start placing photos into templates. The design tools are better than Shutterfly's main app, and the integration with Apple's photo intelligence means it can surface good shots from your library.

The limitation is scope. Motif is Mac-only, so if you're on a phone or PC, it's not an option. The design is template-based — you pick layouts and drag photos in. It's a good tool for people who want some control and are willing to spend an hour or two on the design. But it won't curate thousands of photos for you or create an editorial narrative.

Remeet works the other way around. You upload your entire photo library and the AI handles curation, layout, and pacing. A human designer reviews the result. You get a proof in 72 hours without opening an editor. The trade-off: you give up granular control over every page in exchange for not doing the work.

If you want to stay inside Apple Photos and enjoy the hands-on design process, Motif is a solid choice. If you want a designed book without the design work, that's what Remeet is for.

Frequently asked questions

Does Motif work on iPhone or Windows?

No. Motif is a Mac-only app that runs as an extension inside Apple Photos on macOS. There is no iPhone, iPad, or Windows version. If you need a photo book from a phone or PC, Remeet works from any device with a browser.

Who prints Motif photo books?

Motif is owned by Shutterfly, and all Motif photo books are printed by Shutterfly's facilities. The print quality is comparable to Shutterfly's standard output — solid for everyday books, but not premium archival grade.

Can I use Remeet if my photos are in Apple Photos?

Yes. You can export photos from Apple Photos and upload them to Remeet, or share them via a link. Remeet is not limited to any single platform — it works with photos from any source, including iCloud, Google Photos, Dropbox, or a camera roll.
