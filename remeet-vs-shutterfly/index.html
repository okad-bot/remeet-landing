Shutterfly is the biggest name in DIY photo books. Remeet takes a different approach entirely — we design the book for you.

Choose Shutterfly if:

  • You want the lowest possible price and love hunting for coupons
  • You enjoy the creative process of designing your own book
  • You want a wide variety of themed templates (holidays, milestones, etc.)
  • You need gifts in bulk at a budget-friendly price point
  • You want additional products like calendars, cards, and mugs from the same photos

Choose Remeet if:

  • You have hundreds or thousands of photos and don't want to sort them
  • You want professional editorial design without learning an editor
  • You care more about design quality than finding the best coupon code
  • You've been meaning to make a photo book but haven't found the time
  • You want a premium book you'd put on a coffee table, not just a shelf

The honest take

Shutterfly is the mass-market leader for a reason. They offer incredible value — especially during sales — and their editor has every template you could want. If you enjoy designing and want to keep costs low, Shutterfly delivers a solid product at a price that's hard to beat.

Remeet is a fundamentally different product. You're not paying for printing alone — you're paying for professional curation, editorial layout, chapters, and captions. The result looks like a magazine, not a template. The trade-off is clear: Remeet costs significantly more and gives you less control over individual layout choices.

If your goal is a beautiful book at the best price and you're willing to put in the hours, Shutterfly wins. If your goal is a beautiful book without putting in the hours, that's what Remeet is for.
