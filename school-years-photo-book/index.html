How it works

Send each year's photos

Share first-day photos, class pictures, artwork, awards, and candid school moments. We'll organize them into a consistent spread for each grade.

We design by year

Each school year gets its own section with a uniform layout. Same design language from kindergarten through senior year so the progression feels intentional.

Add pages annually

Start any year and keep adding. Each September, send new photos and we'll extend the book. The design stays consistent across every grade.

Print when you're ready

Print a complete book at any milestone — fifth grade graduation, middle school, or the big senior year finish. Extra copies for grandparents from $79.

What each year includes

  • First day of school photo — The backpack, the outfit, the sign, the nervous smile (or confident strut)
  • Class photo — The official group shot with teacher and classmates
  • School portraits — The individual photos from picture day, gap-toothed grins and all
  • Awards and achievements — Honor roll certificates, spelling bee trophies, science fair ribbons
  • Artwork and projects — The best drawings, paintings, dioramas, and creative work from each year
  • Extracurriculars — Sports teams, dance recitals, band concerts, drama club, scouting
  • Friends — Birthday parties, playdates, the best friend photos that capture each era
  • Last day of school — The bookend to each year, taller and older than September

A book that grows with your child

Start any year. Whether your child is starting kindergarten or already in middle school, the book meets you where you are. Missed the early years? Start now — every year documented is a year saved.

Consistent design across years. The biggest challenge with a K-12 photo book is keeping it visually coherent when it spans 13 years. Our designers use a consistent template that adapts to each year's content while maintaining the same typography, spacing, and editorial feel throughout.

Watch the transformation. There's something extraordinary about seeing a five-year-old and an eighteen-year-old in the same book, year by year, page by page. The growth is incremental in life but dramatic on paper.

Beyond the classroom

A school years book isn't only about school. It's about the person your child is becoming each year. Include:

  • Summer highlights between school years
  • Birthday party photos showing their evolving interests
  • Family vacations that mark each era
  • Hobbies and passions as they emerge and change
  • Quotes and funny things they said at each age
