Shipping & Delivery

Free shipping on every US order

Every printed photo book ships free within the United States. Gift-ready packaging included — no extra wrapping needed.

All delivery times are calculated from the date you approve your proof — not the date you place your order. This gives you complete control over timing.

The full timeline from photos to doorstep

Here's what happens from the moment you share your photos to the moment the book arrives:

  1. Share your photos (Day 1) — Upload from your phone, Google Photos, iCloud, or any shared album. Takes about 5 minutes.
  2. We design your book (Days 1-3) — Our design team curates, arranges, and captions your photos. You receive a digital proof within 72 hours.
  3. Review and approve (Your pace) — Take as long as you need. Request changes, swap photos, edit captions. The clock pauses until you approve.
  4. Printing (5-7 business days) — Your book is printed on archival-quality paper with professional-grade color calibration.
  5. Shipping (7-10 business days standard) — Carefully packaged and shipped to your door or directly to the recipient.

Total typical timeline: About 3 weeks from sharing photos to delivery with standard shipping. Need it sooner? Rush and express options cut the shipping time significantly.

Packaging details

Every Remeet book ships in packaging designed to protect and impress:

Protective outer box

Rigid corrugated mailer built for book-sized shipments. Corner protectors prevent any shifting during transit.

Gift-ready inner wrap

Your book arrives in a kraft gift box with acid-free tissue paper. Hand it to the recipient as-is — no wrapping needed.

Ship to someone else

Ordering a gift? You can ship directly to the recipient. Just provide their address during checkout. We don't include pricing information in the package — only a small card that says "A Remeet book, made for you."

You'll receive tracking information so you know exactly when it arrives.

Order tracking

You'll receive email updates at every step:

  • When your proof is ready for review
  • When your book enters production
  • When your book ships (with tracking number)
  • When your book is delivered

International shipping

We currently ship within the United States only. International shipping to Canada, the UK, EU, and Australia is coming soon. If you're outside the US and want to be notified when we expand, reach out at hello@remeet.cc and we'll let you know as soon as it's available.

Frequently asked questions

What if my book arrives damaged?
Contact us within 7 days at hello@remeet.cc with a photo of the damage. We'll reprint and reship at no charge.

Can I change the shipping address after ordering?
Yes, as long as the book hasn't entered production yet. Email us and we'll update it.

Do you ship to PO boxes?
Yes, standard shipping works with PO boxes. Rush and express require a street address.

Can I pick up locally?
We don't offer local pickup at this time. All orders ship from our print facility.
