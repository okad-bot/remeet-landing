Shipping & Delivery

From your photos to your door

We print hardcover books and ship them to the US, Canada and Europe. Order by December 1 for Christmas delivery in the US and Canada.

Books are printed close to you: orders for the US are printed in the US, for Canada in Canada, for Europe in Europe.

The full timeline

  1. Send your photos. One iCloud or Google Photos link is enough.
  2. First proof in 72 hours. A designer picks the best photos and lays out every page.
  3. Edits, up to a week. Two rounds of edits are included. We take any changes you send within a week of the first proof.
  4. Printing, 3 to 5 working days after you approve the final proof.
  5. Delivery. You get a tracking link when the book ships.

Ship to someone else

Ordering a gift? We can ship straight to the recipient. There's no invoice or price in the parcel.

Frequently asked questions

What if my book arrives damaged?
Write to us within 7 days at hello@remeet.cc with a photo of the damage. We'll reprint and reship at no charge.

Can I change the shipping address after ordering?
Yes, as long as the book hasn't gone to print yet. Email us and we'll update it.

When is the last day to order for Christmas?
December 5 for the US and Canada. That leaves time for the proof, your edits, printing and delivery.
