What makes a Remeet travel book different

Story, not timeline

We don't just put photos in order. We build chapters around moments: the arrival, the food, the unexpected detour, the people you met.

We handle the curation

800 photos become 80-120 carefully selected moments. We remove duplicates, filter out the blurry shots, and keep the ones that capture how the trip felt.

Location-aware captions

We use photo metadata to add place names, dates, and context. You add the stories: "The restaurant the taxi driver swore by."

Coffee-table worthy

Full-bleed spreads, editorial typography, thoughtful pacing. A book that looks like it belongs on a shelf, not in a drawer.

Perfect for

  • The big trip — Europe, Southeast Asia, cross-country road trip. The trips worth remembering in print.
  • Honeymoon — A beautiful keepsake that's more personal than a wedding album.
  • A year of travel — Combine several trips into one annual travel book.
  • Family vacations — The trip the kids will want to look back on in 10 years.
  • Solo adventures — Document your own journey in a book you designed for yourself.

How it works

  1. Share your trip photos — Google Photos album, Drive folder, or upload. No sorting needed.
  2. Add context — Tell us the highlights: "The best day was in Porto" or "Include the cooking class."
  3. Get your proof in 72 hours — A complete designed book ready to review.
  4. Request changes — "Add more food photos" or "Make Lisbon its own chapter."
  5. Print — Premium hardcover, free US shipping.
