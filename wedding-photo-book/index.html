Why Remeet for your wedding book

Not a template

This isn't a drag-and-drop editor with clip art borders. Our designers read your photos, understand the flow of your day, and build a layout that feels like an editorial spread — not a scrapbook.

Every source, one book

Professional photographer, second shooter, photo booth, guests' phones — send it all. We blend every source into one cohesive story so nothing gets left in a forgotten album.

The moments between moments

The best wedding photos aren't always the posed ones. The nervous hands before the ceremony. The grandparents watching from the second row. The dance floor at midnight. We find those moments.

No design work from you

You don't choose layouts, crop photos, or arrange pages. You share your photos and tell us anything important. We handle the rest. You review the proof and request changes in plain language.

What's inside your wedding book

  • The full arc of the day — Getting ready, first look, ceremony, portraits, cocktail hour, reception, late-night dancing. Every chapter flows naturally into the next.
  • The quiet moments — The note you wrote each other. The look your dad gave you before walking down the aisle. The flower girl losing a shoe. Real moments, not just highlight reels.
  • Guest perspectives — Photos from friends and family woven in alongside professional shots. The view from the crowd, not just from the photographer.
  • Details that mattered — The florals, the table settings, the menu, the invitation, the shoes. The things you spent months choosing, preserved in context.
  • Captions and context — Not generic labels, but specific notes: "Your college roommates surprised you with this toast" or "This was the song you almost didn't pick."
  • 80-120+ pages — Weddings have a lot of ground to cover. Your book gets the space it needs to tell the whole story, not a compressed highlight reel.

How it works

1. Share your photos

Send your photographer's gallery link, a Google Photos album, a Dropbox folder — whatever you have. Include guest photos too if you want them. No sorting needed.

2. Tell us what matters

Any must-include moments? People to feature? A chapter for the rehearsal dinner? An opening spread with engagement photos? Just tell us in a few sentences.

3. We design the book

Our designers curate the strongest photos, organize them into chapters, write captions, and build an editorial layout. Your proof arrives in 72 hours.

4. Review and print

Review every page. Request changes in plain language — "swap the cover shot" or "add more reception photos." Once you approve, we print a premium hardcover with archival paper and lay-flat binding.

Why couples wait (and why they wish they hadn't)

Most couples never make a wedding album. The photos come back from the photographer, everyone says "we should really make a book," and then life happens. A year passes. Then two. The photos sit in a gallery link that eventually expires or a hard drive that eventually fails.

The longer you wait, the harder it gets. You forget the name of the florist. You can't remember which cousin gave that toast. The details that made your day yours start to blur. A wedding book preserves those details while they're still sharp — and it only takes five minutes of your time to get started.

Frequently asked questions

How many wedding photos should I send?

As many as you have. Most couples send between 500 and 3,000 photos. We curate the strongest moments and build a cohesive story from them. You don't need to sort or pick favorites — that's our job.

Can I include photos from multiple photographers and guests?

Yes. Send us your professional photographer's gallery, your second shooter's photos, and anything guests captured on their phones. We blend all sources into one seamless book so nothing gets lost in a separate album.

How soon after the wedding should I order?

Anytime. Some couples order within weeks while the memories are fresh. Others come to us a year or more later. Your photos won't expire, and the book will be just as beautiful whenever you're ready. That said — the sooner you start, the more details you'll remember for captions.

Do you include both the ceremony and the reception?

Yes. We design the book to follow the natural arc of your day — getting ready, the ceremony, portraits, cocktail hour, reception, and the late-night moments. Every chapter flows into the next.

Can I add engagement photos or rehearsal dinner shots?

Absolutely. Many couples include engagement sessions, rehearsal dinners, or even photos from when they were dating as an opening chapter. Send everything that feels like part of your story and we'll find the right place for it.

Can you use still frames from our wedding video?

Yes. If there are moments only captured on video — the vows, the first dance, a surprise speech — send us the clips and we can pull high-quality stills to include in the book.
