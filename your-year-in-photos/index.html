The concept: your year, designed

At the end of each year, you share your camera roll with Remeet. We do everything else.

Our AI scans every photo from the past 12 months — thousands of images, all the way back to January. It finds the best shots, groups them by season and event, and builds a complete yearly photo book: chronological chapters, curated moments, editorial design, and captions with dates and places.

You review it. You tweak it. You print it. And then next year, you do it again.

No weekend lost to a drag-and-drop editor. No guilt about the photos sitting unseen on your phone. Just one beautiful annual photo book, every single year.

What you get in your year in photos book

Chronological chapters

Your year organized by season — or by month, if you prefer. Winter holidays, spring adventures, summer vacations, fall routines. Each chapter has its own rhythm and its own opening spread.

Best moments curated

From 5,000 photos, we select the 80-120 that tell the story of your year. Not just the "big" moments — the quiet ones too. Morning light in the kitchen. The dog on the couch. The birthday candles.

Milestones highlighted

First day of school. New home. Wedding. New baby. Graduation. The moments that define a year get dedicated spreads with larger photos and more space to breathe.

Editorial design

Not a template. Every page is designed based on what's in the photos. Full-bleed landscapes, intimate portrait grids, text pages for context. The book flows like a magazine you'd want on your coffee table.

Frequently asked questions

When should I start my year in photos book?

Most people start in November or December, when the year is almost complete. But you can start anytime — even mid-year for a "first half" book or a specific season.

What if I didn't take many photos some months?

That's normal. Not every month is equally photogenic. We'll give quieter months a lighter treatment — maybe a single spread instead of a full chapter. The book reflects your actual year, not an idealized version of it.

Can I combine photos from multiple family members?

Yes, and we encourage it. Mom's phone, Dad's phone, the teenager's Instagram saves. More perspectives make a richer annual photo book. Send us everything.

Is this different from Apple's "Memories" or Google Photos "Year in Review"?

Completely. Those are algorithm-generated slideshows you watch once and forget. This is a designed, printed book with editorial layouts, curated selections, and captions — a physical object that lives on your shelf for decades.

Can I gift a year in photos subscription?

Yes. Give someone one year or an ongoing subscription. We'll reach out to them each December to collect photos and design their book. It's the gift that keeps giving — literally, every year.
